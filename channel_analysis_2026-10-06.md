## Do
1. Pick product categories (not stores) with outside breakout evidence that does not depend on a health hook.
2. Rotate the title construction after about 2-6 uses.
3. Judge each upload at 24 and 72 hours against olive oil (about 2.5K/day) before repeating a format.
4. Check Broken Canada and Canada Alert daily. They rotate formats every 10-21 days.
5. Source every ownership line from a filing or press release (e.g. Kicking Horse = Lavazza 80% since 2017; Ethical Bean = Kraft Heinz since 2018).
6. Link Canadian Counter in NexLev or share Studio retention, so the slump can be diagnosed (CTR vs retention vs browse).

## Don't
1. No more "Refusing to Shop At [store]" or single-store videos for at least 2 weeks (Costco included; it had 3 videos in 17 days).
2. No redos of past food hits. They keep about 16%, e.g. eggs 4,396 vs 121K and ham & bacon 2,989 vs 159.5K.
3. No "Thanksgiving"/"Halloween" in titles. Easter got 1,701 and Christmas snacks 1,947; Canadian turkey and candy lists got 79-155.
4. No "ROBBING You Blind": no candidate brand has a finding that supports it, and the phrase scored 38.5K on shoes but 2,939 on tequila.
5. Don't chase health-driven breakouts. Canadian flour copies got 479 / 54 / 10 after Canada Food Insider's 192K health-scare video.
6. Rejected with numbers:
   - flour, sugar, toilet paper, tariff list, fake-foods sequel, stock-up sequel
   - milk, cross-category ownership list, fun-size chocolate
   - winter boots, winter tires, mattresses, household paper
