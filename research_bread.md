## 3. LABEL DECODES

### 3a. "Whole wheat" ≠ "whole grain" in Canada — THE central decode
Health Canada, verbatim (https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/canada-food-guide/resources/healthy-eating-recommendations/eat-a-variety/whole-grain/get-facts.html):
- "When wheat is milled, parts of the kernel are separated and then recombined to make whole wheat flour."
- "Up to 5% of the kernel can be removed to help reduce rancidity and prolong the shelf life of whole wheat flour."
- "The portion of the kernel that is removed for this purpose contains much of the germ and some of the bran."
- "It is only when all parts of the kernel are used in the same relative proportions as they exist in the original kernel, that the flour can be considered whole grain."
- "Whole wheat flour may have much of the germ removed. Therefore, 100% whole wheat bread may not be whole grain—however, it remains a nutritious choice that provides dietary fibre not found in white bread." **(Quote in full INCLUDING the reassurance line — keeps us inside house rules.)**
- **On-shelf proof pair:** Wonder 100% WW = "Whole wheat flour … Wheat germ" (germ added back) vs Dempster's = "Whole grain whole wheat flour including the germ."

### 3b. Percentage games and "multigrain"
- "100% Whole Wheat" = all flour is whole wheat flour (which may itself lack much of the germ).
- "60% whole wheat" is a real product name (Bon Matin, Metro link §2) — the number is the whole-wheat share; the rest is white flour.
- "Multigrain" ≠ whole grain: Villaggio Rustico Harvest Multigrain leads with "Enriched wheat flour" (§2). Contrast: Country Harvest 14 Grains leads with whole grain wheat flour.
- CFIA grain-claims hub: https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/grain-and-bakery-products — quote the live page on screen (my retrieval paraphrased it).

### 3c. "Enriched" white flour is THE LAW — not a bonus
- CFIA verbatim: "The sale of unenriched white flour or foods containing unenriched white flour is not permitted in Canada" and "All white flour sold in Canada for food use… must be enriched." — https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/grain-and-bakery-products/unenriched-flour
- **FDR B.13.001** (current): white flour must contain per 100 g — **thiamine 0.64 mg, riboflavin 0.40 mg, niacin 5.30 mg, folic acid 0.15 mg, iron 4.4 mg** — https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/c.r.c.,_c._870/page-24.html
- **FDR B.13.022** — enriched white bread per 100 g: thiamine 0.40 mg, riboflavin 0.24 mg, niacin 3.3 mg, folic acid 0.10 mg, iron 2.76 mg (same URL).
- Script line: "When the bag brags 'enriched'… that's not generosity. It's the Food and Drug Regulations."

### 3d. The bread standard moved (Dec 2024)
- Archived **FDR B.13.021 [S] "Bread or White Bread"** verbatim: "Bread or White Bread shall be the food made by baking a yeast-leavened dough prepared with flour and water" + permitted optional ingredients list — https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/c.r.c.,_c._870/section-B.13.021-20120302.html
- Repealed by **SOR/2024-244** (https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p2/2024/2024-12-18/html/sor-dors244-eng.html); standards now in **Canadian Food Compositional Standards Volume 12** (12.1 Flour, 12.2 Breads) — https://inspection.canada.ca/en/about-cfia/acts-and-regulations/list-acts-and-regulations/documents-incorporated-reference/canadian-food-compositional-standards-0. **Pull §12.2.1 live before quoting modern wording** (page too large for automated read). Same "standard moved house" beat as tea/ketchup episodes — running series gag.

### 3e. Long list vs short list
- Traditional bread = flour, water, salt, yeast. Commercial contrast: Wonder White's 15-item list vs a bakery baguette's four. All extras (calcium propionate, sorbic acid, SSL, DATEM, L-cysteine hydrochloride) are **permitted ingredients under the FDR framework** — name them ONLY as legal/label facts, zero health commentary.

### 3f. Best-before dates and the twist-tie legend
- CFIA date-marking verbatim: products "with a durable life of 90 days or less" need a best-before date; "A durable life date is not an indicator of food safety, neither before nor after the date"; selling after the date is not prohibited — https://inspection.canada.ca/en/food-labels/labelling/industry/date-markings-and-storage-instructions
- **Twist-tie colour code** (Mon=blue, Tue=green, Thu=red, Fri=white, Sat=yellow — alphabetical): Snopes — real at *some* bakeries, NOT universal; many use one colour — https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/bread-tag-code/. File as "half-true legend — check the printed date, not the tie."

## 1. THE BREAD PRICE-FIXING SCANDAL (centrepiece)

### 1.1 The arrangement and the window
- ✅ Loblaw/George Weston's own words (July 25, 2024 release): "an industry-wide price fixing arrangement between 2001 and 2015 involving certain packaged bread products," discovered March 2015, "immediately reported… to the Competition Bureau" — https://www.loblaw.ca/en/george-weston-limited-and-loblaw-companies-limited-announce-settlement-of-class-action-lawsuits-concerning-their-involvement-in-historical-industry-wide-bread-price-fixing-arrangement/
- 🟡 Bureau court filings (ITO, public Jan 31, 2018): price raised "at least 15 times," ~7¢ wholesale / 10¢ retail each — internally the **"7/10 convention"** — Globe and Mail https://www.theglobeandmail.com/report-on-business/canada-bread-weston-bakeries-and-major-grocers-behind-alleged-bread-price-fixing-competition-bureau/article37802794/. **ITO = allegations to obtain warrants, not findings of fact — say "the Bureau alleged in court filings."**
- 🟡 **"At least $1.50 was added to the price of a loaf"** — Bureau court documents as reported by CP/BNN (https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/company-news/2024/09/19/a-timeline-of-events-in-the-bread-price-fixing-scandal/) and Global News.

## 2. BREAD LAW

### 2.1 The move (Dec 2024)
- ✅ SOR/2024-244 repealed the FDR Division 13 recipe standards → **Canadian Food Compositional Standards, Volume 12 – Grain and Bakery Products** — https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p2/2024/2024-12-18/html/sor-dors244-eng.html; FDR page now shows "B.13.021 [Repealed, SOR/2024-244, s. 84]" etc.

### 2.2 The bread standards — VERBATIM from CFCS Volume 12 ✅
(https://inspection.canada.ca/en/about-cfia/acts-and-regulations/list-acts-and-regulations/documents-incorporated-reference/canadian-food-compositional-standards-0)
- **12.2.1:** "Bread or White Bread is the food made by baking a yeast-leavened dough prepared with flour and water" + permitted list (salt; shortening/lard/butter/margarine; milk; egg; sweetening agent; malt; ≤2% inactive dried yeast; ≤5% other flours; other parts of the wheat berry; vinegar).
- **12.2.2:** Enriched Bread — enriched flour only; and "where the enriched flour used contains not less than 6% of milk solids, may be described by the common name 'milk bread.'"
- **12.2.3:** "Raisin Bread… contains, of the flour used, **not less than 50% of seeded or seedless raisins**" (raisins ≥50% of flour weight — strict rule, great beat).
- **12.2.4:** "(Naming the percentage) Whole Wheat Bread… **contains not less than 60% whole wheat flour in relation to the total flour used**" — the legal floor behind "60% whole wheat" loaves.
- **12.2.5:** "Brown Bread is bread coloured by the use of whole wheat flour, graham flour, bran or molasses." (Brown bread can be brown from molasses.)
- **12.1.3:** "Whole Wheat Flour… from which a part of the outer bran or epidermis layer may have been separated… contains the natural constituents of the wheat berry to the extent of **not less than 95% of the total weight of the wheat from which it is milled**" — the regulatory root of the 5% rule.
- **12.1.2 (living WWII fossil):** "**Vitamin B White Flour (Canada Approved)**… constitutes not less than 70% of the wheat from which it is milled… contains… **in one pound**…" — the 1942 wartime standard still in the law in 2026, still written in pounds.

### 2.3 Mandatory enrichment — FDR B.13.001 ✅ (see Part 1 §3c for numbers)
- History (🟡 academic — state as regulatory history, never health advice): WWII "Canada Approved" used longer extraction because adding synthetic vitamins was then treated as adulteration; enrichment became **mandatory 1976**; **folic acid added November 1998**. Frame: "the government changed the recipe of every white loaf in the country."

## 4. HISTORY BEATS

### 4.1 Marquis wheat 🟡
- Cross of Red Fife × Hard Red Calcutta (1892, A.P. Saunders, Agassiz BC); **Sir Charles E. Saunders**, Dominion Cerealist, selected **Marquis in 1904** — famously judging kernels by **chewing them into a dough ball**. Matured ~10 days earlier than Red Fife; opened the Prairie wheat belt; by **1918, 20 million acres**. Knighted 1934. — The Canadian Encyclopedia https://www.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/marquis-wheat; AAFC history feature.

### 4.2 WWII "Canada Approved" bread (1942) 🟡→✅
- Wartime program: higher-extraction "Canada Approved Vitamin B" flour/bread (adding synthetic vitamins was then adulteration). ✅ Kicker: the standard survives verbatim as CFCS Vol 12 s. 12.1.2 in 2026.

### 4.3 Sliced bread 🟡
- **Otto Frederick Rohwedder** (jeweller, Davenport, Iowa); first sliced loaf sold **July 7, 1928**, Chillicothe Baking Co., Chillicothe, Missouri; his 1912 prototype burned in a fire. Wonder popularized it in the 1930s — origin of "best thing since sliced bread."
- **US banned sliced bread Jan 18 – Mar 8, 1943** (~47 days, to save wax paper); outcry killed the ban. No Canadian equivalent found — don't invent one.

### 4.4 Wonder 🟡 (see Part 1 §2 for Canadian lineage)
- Named by VP Elmer Cline for the "wonder" of the International Balloon Race at Indianapolis Motor Speedway — hence the balloons. "Builds strong bodies 8 ways" (1950s) → "12 ways" (1960s) — US ad history only.

### 4.5 Canadian brand histories
- **Canada Bread 1911** 🟡: founded by merger of five bakeries incl. **Model Bakery (founded by George Weston)** — yes: George Weston helped found the company that, a century later, pleaded guilty to fixing prices WITH Weston's bakery. Verify merger list vs Bimbo Canada About page before air.
- **Ben's Halifax** 🟡: founded 1907 by Ben Moir (Moir chocolate family); Canada Bread 2002; Halifax bakery closed 2015, 105 jobs (Global https://globalnews.ca/news/1898413/canada-bread-closing-halifax-bakery-105-employees-affected).
- **POM** 🟡: "long said to stand for 'Pride of Montreal'" — no stronger.
- **Gadoua** ⚠️: registry says 1911; company brands "since 1873" — conflict unresolved (see UNVERIFIED). NOTE: Part 1 confirms Gadoua went to Weston 2004 → FGF 2021, so do NOT call it independent.
- **Dempster's** ⚠️: founding story thin (1890s Toronto, heritage blogs) — use 1968 "nation's best-selling brand" milestone instead.

### 4.6 Twist-tie legend 🟡
- Some bakeries day-code ties for stock rotation; NO standardized/regulated system; many use one colour. "Half-true; a stocking tool, not a consumer code; the printed best-before date is what the law actually governs."
