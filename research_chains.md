## 1A. Restaurant Brands International (RBI) — the company behind Tim Hortons

**What it is:** RBI is the parent of Tim Hortons, Burger King, Popeyes and Firehouse Subs. The company's own current description: **"RBI owns four of the world's most prominent and iconic quick service restaurant brands – TIM HORTONS®, BURGER KING®, POPEYES®, and FIREHOUSE SUBS®"** with **"nearly $49 billion in annual system-wide sales and over 33,000 restaurants in more than 120 countries and territories."**
Source (RBI corporate site, About Us): https://www.rbi.com/about-us/default.aspx

**The 2014 merger.** Burger King Worldwide and Tim Hortons Inc. announced their merger August 26, 2014 and closed December 12, 2014, creating RBI. RBI's own launch press release (Dec. 15, 2014): the combined company had **"approximately $23 billion in system sales, over 18,000 restaurants in 100 countries and two strong, thriving, independent brands,"** and **"The new global company will be based in Canada, the largest market of the combined company."**
Source (RBI press release): https://www.rbi.com/English/news/news-details/2014/Worlds-Third-Largest-Quick-Service-Restaurant-Company-Launched-with-Two-Iconic-and-Independent-Brands-Tim-Hortons-and-Burger-King/default.aspx
Completion press release: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/restaurant-brands-international-announces-successful-completion-of-tim-hortons-and-burger-king-transaction-516768441.html

**Deal value:** contemporaneous reporting put it at roughly US$11.5 billion — Gulf News headline: **"Burger King to buy Canada's Tim Hortons for $11.5 billion."** Burger King lined up **$12.5 billion of deal financing**, including roughly **$3 billion in preferred-share financing from Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway** (per Forbes/Nasdaq coverage of the deal).
Sources: https://gulfnews.com/business/retail/king-hortons-burger-king-to-buy-canadas-tim-hortons-for-115-billion-1.1376935 ; https://www.forbes.com/sites/greatspeculations/2014/08/29/burger-king-tim-hortons-cross-border-merger-much-more-than-tax-inversion/

**3G Capital's role.** RBI's launch release: **"3G Capital is expected to own approximately 51% of the new company with the balance of the common shares to be held by current public shareholders."** 3G Capital is the Brazilian-founded investment firm that controlled Burger King. (Script framing: attribute — "RBI said at launch that 3G would own about 51%.")
Source: https://www.rbi.com/English/news/news-details/2014/Worlds-Third-Largest-Quick-Service-Restaurant-Company-Launched-with-Two-Iconic-and-Independent-Brands-Tim-Hortons-and-Burger-King/default.aspx

**Does 3G still hold shares? Yes — but far less.** 3G has been selling down through repeated secondary offerings: an August 2024 secondary offering (SEC Form 424B7) after which **"3G RBH was expected to hold approximately 25.9% of RBI's common shares as of August 1, 2024"**; a further block sale reported at ~US$1.2 billion in 2025 (IFR); and per an amended Schedule 13D summarized by StockTitan, as of August 2026 3G reported **94.37 million exchangeable units, about 21.3%** of Restaurant Brands. (Use as: "securities filings show 3G still holds a stake on the order of one-fifth.")
Sources: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1618756/000119312524198891/d854110d424b7.htm ; https://www.ifre.com/equities/2342354/3g-cuts-restaurant-brands-stake-with-us1.2bn-block ; https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/QSR/schedule-13d-a-restaurant-brands-international-inc-amended-major-shar-db7410eaeb91.html

**The "headquartered in Toronto" question — handle with precision.** This is a two-era story:
- *2014:* the merged parent was set up in Canada. RBI's release: **"The new global company will be based in Canada."** U.S. critics called it a tax inversion. CBC reported that the U.S. lobby group Americans for Tax Fairness published a report titled **"Whopper Of A Tax Dodge"** estimating Burger King **"stands to pocket between $400 million and $1.2 billion in tax savings"** — that is the *group's allegation/estimate*, not a finding; the company disputed that tax was the motivation. Source: https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/burger-king-tim-hortons-deal-skirts-taxes-u-s-group-says-1.2871070
- *FY2023 10-K:* RBI listed a **Toronto corporate headquarters (~65,000 sq ft leased)** and a **Miami U.S. headquarters (~150,000 sq ft leased)**. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1618756/000161875624000020/qsr-20231231.htm
- *Today, RBI's own website says:* **"RBI's principal executive offices are in Miami, Florida. In North America, RBI's brands are headquartered in their home markets where they were founded decades ago: Canada for Tim Hortons and the U.S. for Burger King, Popeyes and Firehouse Subs."** Source: https://www.rbi.com/about-us/default.aspx
- **Script-safe formulation:** "RBI is a Canadian-incorporated company whose shares trade in Toronto and New York — but RBI itself now says its principal executive offices are in Miami, with the Tim Hortons brand headquartered in Canada."

**Popeyes (2017).** RBI acquired Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen in a deal completed March 27, 2017, **for US$1.8 billion** (widely reported; RBI paid $79/share cash).
Source: https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/inside-restaurant-brands-international-s-fast-food-empire (and contemporaneous coverage; RBI IR news archive: https://www.rbi.com)

**Firehouse Subs (2021).** Announced November 15, 2021; Restaurant Dive: "Restaurant Brands International to acquire Firehouse Subs for $1B" — an **all-cash US$1.0 billion** deal, completed December 2021.
Sources: https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/restaurant-brands-international-to-acquire-firehouse-subs-for-1b/610028/ ; https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/financing/restaurant-brands-international-completes-its-purchase-firehouse-subs

## 1B. Recipe Unlimited (formerly Cara Operations) — the Fairfax web

This is the company most Canadians have never heard of that owns the sit-down restaurants they grew up in.

**Founded 1883 — selling newspapers on trains.** Per the company's own timeline: **"1883: The Canada Railway News Company is founded."** The name "Cara" derives from the first letters of "Canada Railway." The company moved through railway and airline catering (Aero Caterers, 1941) into restaurants; the modern Cara Operations Limited dates to 1961 and went public in 1968.
Sources (company timeline): https://www.recipeunlimited.com/en/about/timeline.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recipe_Unlimited

**The brands and when they joined:**
- **Swiss Chalet** — company timeline: **"1954: The first Swiss Chalet opens its doors in Toronto, Ontario serving Rotisserie Chicken as the only entrée."** (Note: founded 1954; Cara *acquired* it later — see 1977.)
- **Harvey's** — founded 1959 (Richmond Hill, ON). Cara's timeline: **"1977: CARA purchases Foodcorp Limited including Swiss Chalet and Harvey's."** So the correct script line is: Swiss Chalet born 1954, Harvey's born 1959, both bought by Cara in 1977.
- **Milestones, Montana's, Kelsey's** — timeline: **"2002: CARA completes purchases of Milestone's, Montana's and Kelsey's restaurant brands."**
- **East Side Mario's** (plus Bier Markt, Prime Pubs, Casey's) — timeline: **"2013: An agreement with Fairfax Financial Holdings makes Prime Restaurants a subsidiary of CARA, bringing East Side Mario's, Bier Markt, Prime Pubs and Casey's into the organization."** (Note the name in that sentence — Fairfax enters the story here.)
- **New York Fries** — "Cara Operations Limited completes acquisition of New York Fries," closed October 31, 2015. Source: https://recipeunlimited.investorroom.com/2015-11-02-Cara-Operations-Limited-completes-acquisition-of-New-York-Fries
- **St-Hubert (2016, $537M)** — Cara announced March 31, 2016 a definitive agreement to acquire **"100% of Group St-Hubert, Quebec's leading full-service restaurant operator as well as a fully-integrated food manufacturer for $537 million"**; completed September 2, 2016. Sources: https://recipeunlimited.investorroom.com/2016-09-02-Cara-completes-acquisition-of-St-Hubert ; https://strategyonline.ca/2016/03/31/cara-operations-buys-st-hubert/
- **The Keg (2018, $200M)** — Cara announced January 23, 2018 it would merge with Keg Restaurants Ltd.; per the deal terms, **"Cara paid to KRL's shareholders, Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited and Mr. Aisenstat, an aggregate purchase price of $200 million comprised of $105 million in cash and 3,801,123 Cara subordinate voting shares"**; closed February 22, 2018. Note the seller: The Keg's long-time owner **David Aisenstat — and Fairfax itself**, which was on both sides of the table. Sources: https://recipeunlimited.investorroom.com/2018-01-23-Cara-Operations-to-merge-with-Keg-Restaurants-Ltd ; https://globalnews.ca/news/3981978/the-keg-swiss-chalet-cara-operations/
- **The 2018 rename** — company timeline: **"CARA becomes Recipe Unlimited Corporation, paying homage to our past and representing the many opportunities to lead the industry in growth and innovation"** (May 2018).
Source: https://www.recipeunlimited.com/en/about/timeline.html

**Milestones — no longer theirs.** **"Recipe Unlimited Completes the Sale of Milestones to Foodtastic"** — announced June 14, 2021, closed September 26/27, 2021. (Price not disclosed in the release — see UNVERIFIED.)
Source: https://recipeunlimited.investorroom.com/2021-09-27-Recipe-Unlimited-Completes-the-Sale-of-Milestones-to-Foodtastic

**Fairfax's take-private (2022).** Bloomberg: **"Fairfax Financial Holdings Ltd. pitched a take-private deal for Recipe Unlimited Corp. valuing the Canadian restaurant owner at about C$1.2 billion, with Fairfax paying C$20.73 per share in cash"** (a 53% premium). Shareholders approved; the transaction closed **October 28, 2022**, and Recipe was delisted from the TSX around November 1, 2022. Fairfax Financial is the Toronto holding company built by Prem Watsa. Fairfax + the founding Phelan family's Cara Holdings took the company private; the cash outlay for the publicly held shares was reported at ~$476 million (Private Capital Journal).
Sources: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2022-08-09/fairfax-proposes-taking-over-recipe-unlimited-at-53-premium ; https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/fairfax-recipe-unlimited-1.6545946 ; https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-recipe-unlimited-closes-deal-to-go-private-with-fairfax-financial/ ; https://privatecapitaljournal.com/fairfax-and-phelan-family-to-take-recipe-unlimited-private-for-476m/

**What Recipe says it is today:** **"Canada's leading full service restaurant company and a nationally recognized franchisor of choice,"** with (per its website) roughly **20 brands and 1,100–1,200+ restaurants**, listing Swiss Chalet, St-Hubert, Harvey's, Montana's, New York Fries, Kelsey's, East Side Mario's, Original Joe's, State & Main, Añejo, The Burger's Priest, The Landing Group, Elephant & Castle, Fresh Kitchen + Juice Bar, The Pickle Barrel, Blanco Cantina, Bier Markt — and Olive Garden (Recipe holds Canadian Olive Garden locations).
Source: https://www.recipeunlimited.com/

**One-liner for the episode:** the same holding company (Fairfax) that owns the insurer of your house effectively owns Swiss Chalet, Harvey's, St-Hubert, The Keg, Montana's, Kelsey's, East Side Mario's and New York Fries — and since October 2022 you can't even buy a share of it directly, because it went private.

## 1C. MTY Food Group — the Montreal food-court empire

**Founder story (attributed to published profiles):** Stanley Ma was born in Hong Kong in 1950, immigrated to Canada in 1968, worked in Montreal — early on as a dishwasher — and, per MTY's own corporate history page: **"Stanley Ma, founder of MTY Group, opens his first restaurant in Montreal: 'Le Paradis du Pacifique'"** (1979, a Polynesian/Chinese restaurant in the Laval area). He built MTY into one of North America's biggest franchisors and remains a major shareholder; he served as CEO until 2018 and stayed on as chairman (Globe and Mail profile: "Stanley Ma: An empire on a plastic tray").
Sources: https://mtygroup.com/en/about-us/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Ma ; https://www.theglobeandmail.com/report-on-business/careers/careers-leadership/stanley-ma-an-empire-on-a-plastic-tray/article557383/

**MTY's own current numbers (Q4 fiscal 2025 press release, February 2026):** **"MTY Group franchises and operates quick-service, fast casual and casual dining restaurants over 80 different banners in Canada, the US and Internationally. Based in Montreal… With 7,080 locations, the many flavors of the MTY Group hold the key to responding to the different tastes and needs of today's consumers as well as those of tomorrow."** Fiscal 2025 full-year: **system sales $5,698,400 thousand (≈ $5.7 billion)** and **revenue $1,190,169 thousand (≈ $1.19 billion)**. At the end of Q4 2025: 7,080 locations, of which 6,831 franchised/under operator agreements and 249 corporate.
Source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/19/3240858/0/en/mty-reports-fourth-quarter-results-for-fiscal-2025.html

Source: https://www.theglobeandmail.com/business/article-mty-food-group-struggles-to-satisfy-its-hunger-for-acquisitions/

- **Valentine** (Quebec casse-croûte chain), **Manchu Wok**, **Yuzu Sushi**, **Cold Stone Creamery (Canada)** — all in MTY's portfolio (see MTY brands/AIF); precise deal dates for these are in the UNVERIFIED list below.

**A&W Canada — THE REVEAL. The American-looking one is actually the Canadian one.** The chain:
- First Canadian A&W drive-in: **Winnipeg, 1956** (A&W's materials: "Established in 1956"; "the second largest burger QSR chain in Canada").
- **1972:** the Canadian operations were sold to **Unilever** — and from that point, per multiple accounts: "A&W restaurants in Canada were initially part of the American A&W restaurant chain until they were sold in 1972 and since then, have been owned and operated in Canada independently and separately from A&W operations in the United States."
- **1995:** management buyout — "In July 1995, Unilever Canada sold A&W Food Services of Canada Inc. to a management buyout group led by company president Jefferson Mooney and five vice-presidents."
- **2002:** the **A&W Revenue Royalties Income Fund** IPO'd on the TSX (an **$83.4-million IPO completed February 15, 2002**); the Fund's structure: "Trade Marks receives royalties equal to 3% of the sales of A&W restaurants in the royalty pool."
- **2024:** the two halves merged. July 22, 2024 announcement: unitholders received **"$37.00 in cash (subject to proration)"** per unit or shares — **"a premium of 30% to the Fund's closing trading price on the TSX on July 19, 2024."** CEO Susan Senecal: **"I'm very excited about combining the two halves of A&W."** Unitholders approved October 8, 2024; final court approval October 11, 2024; the combined **A&W Food Services of Canada Inc.** began trading on the TSX under **"AW"** on **October 18, 2024**. HQ: North Vancouver, B.C. (announcement lists "Suite 300, 171 West Esplanade, North Vancouver"). Royalty Pool at Jan. 2024: **1,047 restaurants**.
- **Verdict: Canadian-owned, Canadian-run, TSX-listed.** The root beer stand that looks like Americana is one of the most Canadian companies on this list.
Sources: https://awincomefund.mediaroom.com/2024-07-22-A-W-Revenue-Royalties-Income-Fund-and-A-W-Food-Services-of-Canada-Announce-Strategic-Combination ; https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/a-amp-w-revenue-royalties-income-fund-and-a-amp-w-food-services-of-canada-announce-successful-closing-of-combination-transaction-828787075.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A%26W_(Canada) ; https://www.lexpert.ca/big-deals/aw-completes-ipo/344126 ; https://www.biv.com/news/retail-manufacturing/fast-food-franchisor-aw-cooks-up-corporate-overhaul-9346393

## 3A. The Tim Hortons app — a formal regulatory FINDING (safe to state as a finding)

**What happened:** On June 1, 2022, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC) and the privacy authorities of Quebec, B.C. and Alberta issued a **joint investigation report** (PIPEDA Findings #2022-001) into the Tim Hortons app. This is a formal regulatory finding — you may state it as such.

**Verbatim findings from the report (priv.gc.ca):**
- **"the App would continually (i.e., as often as every few minutes) track a User's location, including in the 'Background', including when the App was not open."**
- **"when a device was moving, the SDK would generally collect a device's location every 2.5 or 6 minutes"** (depending on app version).
- **"Tim Hortons was collecting, via the App, a User's location every few minutes of every day their device was turned on."**
- The commissioners concluded: **"Tim Hortons did not collect and use the granular location data in question for an appropriate purpose"** — noting the company **"collected the granular location data in question for purposes of delivering targeted advertising"** yet **"never used the data for this identified purpose."**
- The investigation was prompted in part by a National Post journalist who found **"Tim Hortons had recorded my longitude and latitude coordinates more than 2,700 times in less than five months."**
- Tim Hortons' response/agreement: the company agreed to **"delete all granular location data in question, as well as data derived therefrom"** and to establish **"a privacy management program with respect to the App."**
Source (primary): https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/opc-actions-and-decisions/investigations/investigations-into-businesses/2022/pipeda-2022-001/

**The class-action settlement (the coffee-and-donut).** Status: **settled and court-approved; a settlement is a compromise, not a finding of liability.** CBC: "Tim Hortons proposes free coffee and doughnut for users to settle class-action suits over data-tracking app" (July 2022). The court-approved deal (approval communicated to class members as of Feb. 1, 2023): eligible app users — Canadians who used the app between April 1, 2019 and September 30, 2020 and had geolocation collected — received **"a credit that can be used to buy one hot beverage of a retail value of CAD $6.19 and a baked good with a retail value of CAD $2.39."** Tim Hortons also said it would "permanently delete any geolocation information it may have collected between April 1, 2019 and Sept. 30, 2020, and direct third-party service providers to do the same."
Sources: https://www.cbc.ca/news/business/tim-hortons-app-1.6536175 ; https://mobilesyrup.com/2023/02/01/tim-hortons-app-settlement-free-coffee-donut/ ; https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2023/02/01/free-tim-hortons-coffee-and-donut-now-available-from-app-privacy-settlement/

**Script-safe framing:** "Canada's privacy watchdogs *found* the app tracked users' locations as often as every few minutes — that's their finding, quoted. Tim Hortons agreed to delete the data. Separate class actions were *settled* — settlements aren't admissions — and the compensation was, famously, one hot drink and one baked good."
