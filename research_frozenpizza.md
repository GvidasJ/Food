# PART 2 — REGULATION & INDUSTRY (agent dossier, 2026-08-26)

## a. The cheese law — Canada legally has TWO mozzarellas (VERIFIED VERBATIM)
- Standards lived in FDR B.08.033; repealed by SOR/2024-244 s.55 (Dec 2024), moved intact to **CFCS Table 7.4.2** — "same law, new address."
- **The table:** Mozzarella (Scamorza): **max 52.0% moisture / min 20.0% milk fat**. Part Skim Mozzarella: 52/15. **Pizza Mozzarella: 58.0% / 15.0%**. **Part Skim Pizza Mozzarella: 61.0% / 11.0%.** → "Pizza Mozzarella" may carry 6 points more water, 5 points less fat; the part-skim version can be **61% water with 11% fat** vs real Mozzarella's 52/20.
- **The casein carve-out (B.08.033(1)(a)(i.1) verbatim in dossier):** protein from actual milk/cream must be ≥**83%** for Mozzarella — but only ≥**63% for Pizza Mozzarella** — the rest may come from "other milk products." 🔥 This is the legal doorway for "modified milk ingredients."
- **FAIRNESS (mandatory):** Pizza Mozzarella is NOT fake — it's a real standardized cheese engineered to melt/brown on pizza; the point is that the word "Pizza" before "Mozzarella" changes the legal recipe and almost nobody knows.
- **"Modified milk ingredients" legal definition (B.01.010(3)(b) item 7.1 verbatim):** "calcium reduced skim milk…, casein, caseinates, cultured milk products, milk serum proteins, ultrafiltered milk, whey, whey butter, whey cream and any other component of milk the chemical state of which has been altered from that in which it is found in milk." One phrase, a dozen possible ingredients — all DAIRY, not chemicals.
- **Processed-cheese ladder:** Processed Cheese → **"Processed Cheese Food" = minimum 51% cheese** (max 46% moisture) → spreads. Ladder graphic: Mozzarella → Pizza Mozzarella → Processed Cheese → Cheese Food → modified-milk builds.
- ⚠️ Reg agent found a **Delissio Stuffed Crust listing**: "part skim pizza mozzarella cheese (modified milk ingredients, bacterial culture, salt…)" vs Giuseppe's "mozzarella cheese (milk…)" — THE on-camera contrast, but VERIFY ON PHYSICAL PACK (listing-sourced).
- Simulated meat (B.01.100): "simulated" + "contains no meat" required on standalone products, but NOT on the front when used as an ingredient of another food (pizza) — ingredient-list-only disclosure; know-where-to-look item, not scandal.
- "Wood-fired," "stone-baked," "artisan": **no legal definition anywhere** — only FDA s.5(1)/SFCA s.6(1) don't-mislead.
- Origin claims: **"Product of Canada" = ~98% Canadian**; **"Made in Canada" = last substantial transformation here + qualifier** ("from domestic and imported ingredients"). ⚠️ NEVER say "51% rule" for food (that's non-food Competition Bureau guidance). Dr. Oetker's own site: Ristorante (non-specialty), Giuseppe, Casa di Mama "made right here in Ontario" — a factory claim, not an ingredient claim.

## b. FOP "High in sodium" symbol (in force Jan 1, 2026)
- Pizza "consumed as a meal" reference amount = **200 g** (updated Oct 2024 table) → main-dish threshold **30% DV = 690 mg**. Typical frozen pizzas ≈450–550 mg/100 g → ~900–1,100 mg per 200 g → most classic pepperoni/deluxe pizzas should now carry the symbol (often + "High in saturated fat"). ⚠️ THIS IS DERIVED ANALYSIS — present as "how the rule works" and CONFIRM ON 2026 BOXES (the payoff shelf shot). Snack formats (pockets/pops) face lower thresholds.
- Sodium context (neutral): Canadians average ~2,760 mg/day vs 2,300 limit; 58% over; "bakery products, mixed dishes, and processed meats account for half" (HC 2017/2018). Landing: the label now does the scanning; check the serving fraction (¼–⅓ of pizza).

## c. Dairy economics (attributed, neutral)
- Supply management para: quotas, administered class pricing, **245.5% over-quota cheese tariff** (Globe). Fairness: stability + no taxpayer subsidy; frame as trade-off.
- 🔥 **The pizza-kit loophole (2012–13):** US "pizza topping kits" (~80% mozzarella + 20% pepperoni) ruled duty-free "food preparations" — chains legally dodged the 245.5% tariff; dairy farmers estimated (attribute) up to 12% of fresh-pizza cheese market; Ottawa closed it **Nov 29, 2013** — CBSA memo literally titled "Tariff Classification of Pizza Kits."
- 🔥 **Class 3(d) (June 1, 2013):** discounted milk (~$0.72/kg reduction per CDC) for mozzarella on FRESH pizzas at registered restaurants ONLY — frozen-pizza makers not eligible; they lean on the Pizza Mozzarella standard + modified milk ingredients instead. "Canada built two relief valves for pizza cheese: restaurants got a discount; factories got a second mozzarella."
- Diafiltered milk/Class 7 saga in one neutral sentence; USMCA killed Classes 6/7 (2020).
- Cheese stats (AAFC 2025 profile): 2024 production 528,207 t; **cheddar 161,317 t #1; mozzarella 153,883 t #2** (⚠️ never say mozzarella is #1; it out-produced cheddar only in some months, e.g. Mar 2022); per-capita 15.24 kg; retail $7.2B; cheese CPI +18% 2020–24; big three: Agropur, Lactalis, Saputo.
- Market size: US$824.4M (2024) — "industry analysts estimate" (Market Research Future), attribute.

## d. History (verified)
- **Delissio:** Kraft trademark on Canadian frozen pizza since the 1980s; when Kraft's DiGiorno (US 1995) came north in **1999**, Kraft used the Delissio name — twin slogans "It's not delivery, it's DiGiorno/Delissio." **2010: Nestlé bought Kraft's frozen pizza business for US$3.7B.** **Feb 1, 2023:** Nestlé Canada announced 6-month wind-down/exit (Delissio, Stouffer's, Lean Cuisine, Life Cuisine); CEO John Carmichael verbatim: **"This decision enables us to further invest in priority categories. We remain open to enhancing our portfolio where it makes sense for our business."** No Canadian factory existed. No credible reporting of licensing/relaunch through Aug 2026; retailer pages likely stale. Global News context: price crept toward ~$7 from a one-time ~$3 pizza.
- **Dr. Oetker London ON:** development from 2011, **opened May 2014**, ~$100M incl. **$19M government grants**; 10th anniversary (2024): **~430 employees, ~400,000 pizzas/DAY** — North American hub. 2018 expansion.
- **McCain flip:** **Aug 2014** Dr. Oetker bought McCain's North American frozen pizza business (incl. Ellio's US, plants Grand Falls NB + Lodi NJ) with a **2-year McCain-brand licence (Pizza Pockets excluded)**; **2015 rebrand to Giuseppe Pizzeria**; ⚠️ deal price NOT disclosed — never quote one.
