# PART 1 — BRANDS & LABELS (verbatim; re-read every physical package on camera)

## 1. SCHNEIDERS — the claimed #1, Kitchener 1890, owned by Maple Leaf since 2004
- History (verified): J.M. Schneider, injured hand at a Kitchener button factory ($1/day), starts sausage at home 1886; company launched **1890** (Kitchener, then called Berlin). Strategy magazine (Jan 13, 2017), quoting Maple Leaf SVP: **"Red Hots, introduced in 1928, is Canada's number one hot dog brand"**; "Schneiders has been first or second in market share... for many years" (2017, company-sourced — present as dated + attributed).
- Ownership arc (verified via SEC 8-K + Western Producer): family-controlled until **1998** → Maple Leaf's hostile ~$130M bid REJECTED; family sells to **Smithfield Foods (Virginia)** Nov 1998 (63%), completed Nov 2001 → **April 5, 2004: Smithfield sells Schneider Corp to Maple Leaf Foods ~$378M**. Irony beat: Maple Leaf lost the bidding war, bought it anyway six years later.
- **Red Hots Original** verbatim (schneiders.ca): "Pork, Water, Modified corn starch, Salt, Potassium lactate, Sodium phosphate, Sodium erythorbate, Sodium diacetate, Sugar, Sodium nitrite, Spice extract, Garlic powder, Smoke" — 350 mg sodium / 38 g wiener (~920 mg/100 g). "A tradition at Schneiders since the 1940s." No MSM; corn starch filler; sodium nitrite.
- **Juicy Jumbos Original** verbatim: "Pork, Chicken, Water, Sugars (dextrose; corn syrup solids), Salt, Modified vinegar, Sodium phosphate, Garlic powder, Sodium erythorbate, Spice extracts, Sodium nitrite, Natural flavour, Smoke" — **700 mg sodium per 75 g wiener** (~930/100 g). No starch.
- **All Beef Wieners** verbatim: "Beef, Water, Sugars (corn syrup solids; dextrose), Modified corn starch, Salt, Potassium lactate, Sodium phosphate, Sodium diacetate, Sodium erythorbate, Garlic powder, Spice extractives, Sodium nitrite, Flavour, Smoke" — 340 mg/38 g. "All beef" still carries corn starch.
- Prices at time of research (search-snippet renders — confirm): Red Hots 375 g **$3.97 rollback (reg $7.27)** Walmart.ca; Juicy Jumbos 450 g $4.97 rollback (reg $9.96).

## 2. MAPLE LEAF — the "natural" flagship and the celery-extract story
- **Natural Top Dogs Original** verbatim (mapleleaf.ca): "Chicken, Pork, Water, Tapioca starch, Sea salt, Modified vinegar, Spice (with garlic & onion), **Cultured celery extract**, Cherry powder, Smoke" — 370 mg sodium / 38 g (16% DV; ~970/100 g — HIGHER than several MSM value dogs). Pack claims: "Gluten Free," "Filler Free," "Made with natural ingredients." (Metro's render differs slightly — tapioca/cane sugar discrepancy; READ CURRENT PACKAGE.)
- **THE KEY STORY (attributed, no health claims):** no "sodium nitrite" on label — "cultured celery extract" instead. Maple Leaf's own ingredient-promise page lists sodium nitrite under "We promise never to use" while approving Cultured Celery Extract (mapleleaf.ca/ingredients). **CBC Marketplace (Feb 2, 2012):** lab-tested Natural Selections; reported the cultured celery extract "is actually a nitrite"; expert quote: **"For all intents and purposes it is bio-chemically identical."** Maple Leaf then said it "would change its Natural Selections labels to include the fact that the products contain nitrite," and noted labels "were developed in conjunction with the Canadian Food Inspection Agency." **Canadian Grocer (May 9, 2018):** ~100 SKUs reformulated to cultured celery extract; campaign line: "If you can't spell it, you won't find it in our food."
- Corporate: Maple Leaf Foods, **Mississauga, ON**; formed 1991; **Wallace McCain** acquired control 1995; **Michael McCain** CEO ~25 years, stepped down **May 11, 2023** (Curtis Frank now CEO, McCain executive chair).
- No current Maple Leaf-BRAND hot dog lists MSM (post-2018 reformulation) — but the PORTFOLIO still sells MSM-first wieners (Hygrade below; Schneiders foodservice roller-grill wieners list "Mechanically separated chicken" first). Brand-scope contrast is verified and fair as label fact.
- "Less Salt" Natural Top Dogs variant exists. Price at time of research: **$7.99 / 375 g** Superstore (snippet — confirm). Note: the "natural" dog costs ~2x the Schneiders rollback and ~3x no name.

## 4. QUEBEC: LAFLEUR (Olymel) & HYGRADE (Maple Leaf)
- **Lafleur Original Wieners** — owner **Olymel** (Quebec gov't food directory), majority shareholder **Sollio Cooperative Group** (Saint-Hyacinthe). Verbatim (lafleur.com EN): "**Mechanically separated meats (chicken and/or turkey)**, Pork and/or beef, Water, **Wheat flour**, Salt, Modified corn starch, Hydrolyzed plant protein (soya), Spices, Onion powder, Ground mustard, Sodium erythorbate, Onion extracts, Sodium nitrite, Smoke flavour, Garlic powder, **Milk ingredients (milk)**, Canola oil, Sugars, Sodium phosphate, Smoke." **20 ingredients — busiest label in the dossier.** 320 mg/38 g (QC gov directory; allergens wheat/milk/mustard/soy). $4.99 sale (reg $5.99)/450 g IGA flyer.
- **Hygrade Wieners Original 450 g** — owner **MAPLE LEAF FOODS** (2009 recall notice groups "Maple Leaf, Shopsy's and Hygrade Brand Wieners" under Maple Leaf Consumer Foods; Strategy 1993: acquired from Unox/Unilever). Verbatim (iga.ca): "**Mechanically Separated Chicken**; Water; Pork; Salt; Modified Corn Starch; Modified Vinegar; Sodium Phosphate; Spice; Spice Extract; Sodium Erythorbate; Garlic Powder; **Sodium Nitrite**; Smoke" — 330 mg/38 g. TENSION BEAT (label fact, not health claim): parent Maple Leaf promises "never" sodium nitrite in Maple Leaf-BRAND food, while its Hygrade label lists it. $5.00–6.49 sale/450 g Maxi/Super C.
- Montreal steamie culture: Lesters/Lafleur named as standard steamie brands (Wikipedia "Montreal hot dog").
- **Lesters All Beef Wieners** (QC gov directory, French verbatim): beef first, then "amidon de maïs modifié, farine de blé, produit protéique de soya, gluten de blé…" — **an "all beef" label carrying wheat flour, wheat gluten, soy protein AND corn starch; 430 mg/38 g (~1,130/100 g — saltiest per-100g in dossier).** Myth-bust: "all beef" ≠ "nothing but beef." ## 6. COSTCO — the anchor
- **Kirkland Signature Beef Wieners** retail pack VERIFIED in Canada: Costco Business Centre "Pack of 14, 1.72 kg" — listing claims: "All-beef wieners," "No meat by-products or corn syrup," "Gluten-free," and **"Wieners served at the Costco Food Court."** Ingredients (Costcuisine blog transcription — RE-READ AT WAREHOUSE): "beef, water, dextrose, salt, spices, sodium lactate, garlic, sodium diacetate, sodium erythorbate, paprika and extractive, sodium nitrite" — no MSM, no filler. **1,230 mg sodium per ~123 g quarter-pound wiener (~1,000/100 g — comparable per-100g; SAY THE GRAM SIZE).** ≈$15.99/1.72 kg (2022-vintage price — confirm).

## SCORECARD HIGHLIGHTS (label facts + our arithmetic)
- MSM on label: Lafleur (first), Hygrade (first), Compliments (first), Great Value (2nd), Maple Lodge (first), Mina (first). NOT on: Schneiders retail, Maple Leaf brand, PC Free From, Kirkland, Grimm's, Freybe, Piller's, Harvest, Butterball, no name (per OFF — verify).
- COUNTER-INTUITIVE (safe as label math): value MSC dogs often show LOWER sodium per 38 g than premium "natural" ones — Compliments 300 mg vs Maple Leaf Natural 370 mg; celery-cured ≠ lower sodium.
- Price ladder at time of research: no name ~$2.50/450 g → Compliments ~$3 → GV $4.97/900 g → Lafleur $4.99 → Hygrade $5-6.50 → PC Free From $6.29-7.99/375 g → Maple Leaf Natural $7.99/375 g. The "natural" dog costs ~3x the cheapest.
