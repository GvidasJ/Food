**no name Apple Juice from Concentrate:** *"Water, Concentrated apple juice, Ascorbic acid"* — three ingredients.

## DECODES
- **"And/or" recipes** — "apple and/or grape," "apple and/or pear and/or grape": the recipe floats with commodity prices, the label legally hedges.
- **Vitamin C ≠ fruit** — Tang and Jammers advertise vitamin C at 0% juice.
- **"From concentrate"** = water removed for shipping, restored at bottling; sugar identical either way.
- **Cheap-juice base** — "berry"/"tropical" products run on apple/grape/pear: SunRype Wildberry (apple and/or grape first), Fruitopia (pear), Ocean Spray Cran-Raspberry (apple), Fruité (apple/grape only).
- **Lassonde's own site** states drinks/cocktails/punches are *"made using water… have added sugar"* and (per FAQ capture) that printing % juice is **not required** — while SunnyD (5%) and Arizona (10%) voluntarily print theirs.
- **Diet creep**: sucralose/stevia now in SunnyD, Fruité, SunRype Slim, Jammers — "less sugar" = artificially sweetened, not more fruit.

## 🔥 THE FROZEN-CAN SAGA (very Canadian arc)
Lassonde (Old South) exited frozen concentrate ~2025 (waning demand) → **Coca-Cola announced Jan 30 2026 it would kill Minute Maid/Five Alive/Fruitopia frozen cans by April 2026** → **Loblaw brought the can back under no name in June 2026 — but the lineup is lemonades and PUNCHES, from a non-Canadian supplier.** Nobody manufactures frozen juice concentrate in Canada anymore.

## OWNERSHIP MAP
- **Lassonde Industries** (Rougemont QC, TSX LAS.A, founded 1918, family-controlled): **CA$2.60B revenue 2024, ~2,900 employees.** Brands: **Oasis, Rougemont, Fruité, Allen's, Fairlee, Graves, Kiju Organic, Hydra+, SunRype, Del Monte Canada (marketed), Old South (exited)** + US brands. **The Canadian juice aisle is substantially one Quebec family company** — and it's the genuine "Canadian winner" of the video.
- Coca-Cola: Simply, Minute Maid, Five Alive, Fruitopia. Killed Odwalla 2020.
- **Tropicana**: PepsiCo sold 61% to PAI Partners (~$3.3B, Aug 2021). US shrinkflation 52→46 oz + redesign rout; lost #1 to Simply. ⚠️ US story — Canadian formats differ.
- Kraft Heinz: Tang, Kool-Aid. Campbell's: V8 (⚠️ Canadian V8 Splash label not retrieved).
- Co-ops: Ocean Spray; Welch's = National Grape Co-operative incl. **Ontario growers**.
- Anecdote: **Lassonde vs Olivia's Oasis (2005–2012)** — sued a small Quebec soap maker over "Oasis," won at appeal, then paid her costs within days after a social-media firestorm.

## RECALLS / ENFORCEMENT
- **Great Value 100% Grape Juice** recalled Nov 2025 — **tartrate crystals** (naturally occurring, harmless quality issue — honest framing available).
- JustJuice Organic Pineapple recalled June 2024 — mould.
- 🔥 **CFIA food-fraud juice testing:** 2023–24 **94% satisfactory (130/138)**; 2024–25 **88% (151/172)** — failures for foreign sugars/dilution/cheaper-juice substitution (adulterated pomegranate, citrus). "Canada tests juice for fraud, and ~1 in 10 targeted samples fails."
- Quebec **Disney-characters class action** (Rivard v. Lassonde et al., 2016) — discontinued 2017 after companies dropped the cartoon characters.
- ⚠️ US-only, both DISMISSED: Simply PFAS suit (dismissed twice), V8 Splash suit. If ever used, state dismissals.

## OJ PRICE/TRADE STORY
- OJ futures peaked ~US$4.95/lb May 2024 (citrus greening; worst Florida crop since WWII).
- **Canada's 25% counter-tariff on US OJ (March 4 2025) → US OJ import value down ~64%; orange imports at a 20-year low.**
