Think about that as an absolute price point, which is more important for that consumer to get them into the restaurants than maybe value messaging."** Nation's Restaurant News reported he described lower-income consumers earning roughly $45,000 and below reducing transactions, likely choosing to eat at home as grocery inflation cooled faster than restaurant prices.
