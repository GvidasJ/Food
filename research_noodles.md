## For Canadian Counter — "Instant Noodles" episode (counter/label-law spine)
### Research date: August 28, 2026. All prices "at time of research" — re-verify on camera.

**Reference video:** Consumer Exposed instant noodles (whhFoLtWtsw, ~193K views, 4.4x outlier). Reference spine runs on health claims (oxidized oil, microplastics, ethylene-oxide cancer framing, medical sodium advice) — ALL OFF-LIMITS under house style. Our spine: label law (FOP "High in sodium" symbol mandatory Jan 1, 2026; half-block serving trick; TBHQ/MSG legally listed), ownership reveals, recalls reported factually and attributed, Canadian-wheat economics close.

# PART 1 — BRANDS & LABELS (verbatim; re-read every physical package on camera)

## 1. MR. NOODLES — the "Canadian" brand that isn't made in Canada
- Founded 1970s by **Anderson Watts Ltd. of Vancouver, B.C.** Per Wikipedia: "Packages are imported by Canadian products supplier Anderson Watts Ltd. of Vancouver and manufactured by partner Beltek Foods of Huizhou, China"; company "owned by both Beltek Foods and Anderson Watts Limited." (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Noodles)
- **No Canadian plant.** Canadian-owned brand, Chinese manufacture. This is the episode's first ownership reveal.
- Verbatim — Chicken, 85 g (Dizin.ca listing, cross-checked Open Food Facts 059491000501):
  - Noodles: "enriched wheat flour, palm oil, salt, sugar, guar gum, garlic powder"
  - Soup base: "salt, artificial chicken flavour, monosodium glutamate, sugar, yeast extract powder (dried bread yeast, water), turmeric powder, white pepper powder, disodium inosinate and disodium guanylate, dried leek, caramel"
- **Half-package serving trick: YES.** Label serving = 43 g (½ package): **980 mg sodium (41% DV)** → **~1,960 mg per 85 g package**. CONFLICT FLAG: an OFF entry shows ~600 mg/43 g (label vintage?) — READ CURRENT PACKAGE ON CAMERA before quoting the number.
- Fried in palm oil (first fat in block). No TBHQ (tocopherols era lists none).
- "Less Sodium" flat-noodle line exists (48×85 g, Costco Business Centre Canada) — exact mg not captured, read on pack.
- Price: **$0.69** chicken 85 g Loblaws.ca; $0.39–0.99 typical at No Frills/Superstore (Aug 28, 2026).
- DO NOT SAY: "Elco Fine Foods" (unverified — the owner is Anderson Watts + Beltek).

## 2. SAPPORO ICHIBAN — the prairie/BC institution, made in California
- Parent: **Sanyo Foods**, family-owned, Gunma prefecture, Japan, est. 1953. "Sanyo Foods Corp. of America was established in 1978 in Garden Grove, California, and has been manufacturing Sapporo Ichiban instant noodles in California for 40 years." (Ramen Rater interview w/ Sanyo, theramenrater.com/2018/05/07)
- **No Canadian plant** — North American product is Garden Grove, CA.
- Western-Canada story: Canadian distributor **Toyo Importing Co. Ltd.**: "Our first order of 25 cases was purchased in 1969; today we sell in excess of 800,000 cases in Western Canada alone" — "a household name and a leader among all instant noodles in the Canadian marketplace." (toyoimporting.com)
- Verbatim — Original 100 g (OFF 0076186010003; OCR artifacts "Extracted flour"/"Seasame" — CONFIRM WORDING ON PACK):
  - Noodles incl.: "**Palm oil (TBHQ and citric acid added to protect flavor)**", tapioca starch, soy sauce (w/ sodium benzoate), guar gum, sodium carbonate, potassium carbonate
  - Soup base: "Salt, Monosodium glutamate, Sugars…, Caramel colour (sulphites)…, Disodium succinate…, Disodium inosinate, Disodium guanylate…"
- **Sodium: 1,820 mg per 100 g package — serving IS the whole package** (no half-block trick). Shio flavour ~2,090 mg/pkg (MyFoodDiary).
- Fried in palm oil, **TBHQ named on-label** — the episode's cleanest TBHQ read.
- Price: **$5.58/5-pack** Walmart.ca; singles ~$1.29–1.49 Superstore (Aug 28, 2026).

## 3. NONGSHIM SHIN RAMYUN — "second largest market share in Canada" (their claim)
- Nongshim Co., Seoul. **Nongshim America**: Rancho Cucamonga, CA — first plant 2005, second 2022, ~850M units/yr; "exports to Canada, Mexico…" (nongshimusa.com/about). **Nongshim Canada Inc.** registered Oct 7, 2019, Mississauga ON + Delta BC. Nongshim global site: after Toronto sales office (2002) it "grew to have the second largest market share in the Canadian instant noodle market" (eng.nongshim.com).
- BOTH origins on Canadian shelves (California-made and Korea-made via Asian-grocery channel) — **on camera: read the "Product of" line on the specific bag.**
- Verbatim — US-format 4-pack label (OFF 0031146022861): enriched wheat flour, "palm oil, potato starch, modified potato starch, salt, contains less than 2% of each of the following: beef extract, beef fat…, **hydrolyzed corn protein, hydrolyzed soy protein**…, tocopherols (antioxidant), yeast extract…"
- **No "monosodium glutamate" as such on the US label** — glutamates arrive via hydrolyzed proteins + yeast extract. Same label-reading moment as Cup Noodles.
- **Half-bag serving trick: YES — "0.5 bag (60 g)"**; full bag (120 g) = **1,790 mg sodium** (Nongshim USA nutrition data).
- Fried in palm oil. No TBHQ (tocopherols).
- Price: Walmart.ca 4-pack **$4.44 rollback (reg. $5.97)**; Shin cups 6×75 g at Costco Business Centre. DO NOT quote Costco 18-pack price (found figure was US Costco, USD).

## 4. SAMYANG BULDAK — the TikTok-era riser
- **Samyang Foods**, Seoul, founded **1961**; made **Korea's first instant ramen in 1963**; Buldak launched **2012**. Canadian stock **"Made in South Korea."**
- Verbatim — Hot Chicken 140 g (OFF 8801073141896): Noodle (76.7%): "wheat flour(52%), tapioca starch(12%), palm oil(11%), wheat gluten, salt, soybean oil, water, acidity regulator[potassium carbonate(e501), sodium carbonate(e500), sodium phosphate dibasic(e339), sodium polyphosphate(e452), citric acid(e330)], thickener [guar gum(e412)], emulsifier[lecithin(e322)], colour[riboflavin(e101)]"; Soup (22.7%) incl. "artificial chicken flavor powder… yeast extract powder…"
- **Sodium: 1,640 mg per 140 g package** (whole pack = one serving; stir-fry format, sauce mostly consumed). No TBHQ, no MSG-as-such (yeast extract).
- **Denmark recall — precise, attributed framing:** June 2024, Denmark's Veterinary and Food Administration recalled **three** Buldak products (3x Spicy, 2x Spicy, Hot Chicken Stew), saying capsaicin levels "so high that there is a risk of acute poisoning" (Time). **July 2024: partially reversed** after DTU National Food Institute reassessment — 2x Spicy and Hot Chicken Stew returned; **3x Spicy remained recalled** (NBC). Partly based on the company's own marketed spiciness figures. **No Canadian (CFIA) recall — legal on every Canadian shelf.** MANDATORY: report as Danish regulatory action, partially withdrawn; never as a danger claim.
- Price: Walmart.ca 5-packs Original/Carbo **$8.97** (Aug 28, 2026).

## 5. NISSIN — the inventor's company; Canadian shelves carry two streams
- Momofuku Ando "developed Chicken Ramen, the first-ever instant noodles, on August 25, 1958" (history.com); **Cup Noodles 1971**.
- US plants: Gardena CA (since 1972), Lancaster PA; third plant (~$228M, South Carolina) announced Nov 2023. **No Canadian plant.**
- Two streams in Canada: (a) **Nissin Foods USA Cup Noodles** (US-made) at Walmart etc.; (b) **Nissin Hong Kong "Demae Ramen"** (Made in Hong Kong) at T&T/Asian grocers.
- Verbatim — Cup Noodles Chicken 64 g (nissinfoods.com, US label): "ENRICHED FLOUR…, PALM OIL, SALT…, HYDROLYZED CORN PROTEIN, HYDROLYZED SOY PROTEIN…, SODIUM TRIPOLYPHOSPHATE…, **TBHQ (PRESERVATIVE)**, WHEAT."
- **Sodium: 1,160 mg per 64 g cup (one serving = container).**
- KEY BEAT: current Cup Noodles marketing flashes **"No MSG"** while the list names hydrolyzed corn + soy protein and yeast extract — regulatorily accurate, and exactly the kind of claim CFIA calls potentially misleading (Part 2). Read both on camera.
- Demae Ramen Sesame (HK): E-number label, "Flavour Enhancer (621, 635)" = MSG openly; antioxidants 307b/304 (not TBHQ).
- Nissin Chow Mein trays: Walmart.ca listing shows unavailable — treat as limited/possibly discontinued in Canada, don't assert either way.

## 6. PRIVATE LABEL — no name / Great Value / Compliments (PC makes none)
- **no name Chicken Instant Noodle Soup 85 g** (Loblaws.ca verbatim): Noodles: "Enriched Wheat Flour, Palm Oil (contains Tocopherols), Water, Salt". Seasoning: "Salt, Sugars (corn Maltodextrin, Sugar), Enriched Wheat Flour, **Monosodium Glutamate**, Yeast, Artificial Chicken Flavour, Palm Oil, **Hydrolyzed Soy Protein**, Turmeric, Onion Powder, Silicon Dioxide, Disodium Inosinate, Disodium Guanylate, Dried Chives."
  - **Sodium: 1,360 mg per 85 g package — and the serving IS the whole package. No half-block trick.** The 39-cent brand is more honest about the serving than brands at 3x the price.
  - **Price: $0.39** at Real Canadian Superstore (Aug 28, 2026) — likely the cheapest instant noodle in Canada.
- **Great Value (Walmart) chicken ramen** — cup 64 g verbatim (OFF 0628915787740): "…**monosodium glutamate**…, **hydrolyzed soy protein**…" — **710 mg sodium per 64 g cup.** (Only the cup panel verified verbatim; pull the 3×85 g pack panel in-store.)
- **Compliments Chicken Instant Noodles 65 g cup** (compliments.ca verbatim): "Enriched wheat flour, Palm oil…, **Monosodium glutamate**…" — **800 mg per 65 g cup (33% DV).**
- All three: fried in palm oil, tocopherols not TBHQ, MSG listed openly. Both no name and GV list MSG **and** hydrolyzed soy protein.
- **President's Choice makes no instant noodle block** (only ramen broth/udon) — "PC doesn't currently make one" with caveat.
- DO NOT name a co-packer for any store brand (not disclosed anywhere found).

## 7. INDOMIE MI GORENG — Indofood, Indonesia; five sachets, TBHQ twice
- Made by **Indofood CBP**, Jakarta; Canadian packs product of Indonesia. On-shelf name is literally "Instant **Fried** Noodles."
- Verbatim — Mi Goreng 85 g export label (OFF 0089686010947): "NOODLE: Wheat flour, vegetable oil, tapioca starch, acidity regulator (500(i), 501(i)), stabilizer (452(i), 412), **antioxidant (319)** and colouring (102). SEASONING POWDER: Sugar, salt, **flavor enhancer (621)**…, synthetic chicken flavor (contain flavor enhancer (631, 627))… CHILI SAUCE: …preservatives (211, 223). FRIED ONION (contain antioxidant (319))."
  - **Antioxidant 319 = TBHQ (E319) — appears twice** (noodle + fried onion). Colouring 102 = tartrazine. Flavor enhancer 621 = MSG.
- **Sodium: 869 mg per 85 g pack** (whole-pack serving) — actually among the lower packs; the honest surprise beat.
- Price: Walmart.ca 5×85 g **$3.97**; cup $1.47 (Aug 28, 2026).

## 8. MARUCHAN — America's giant, marginal in Canada
- Brand of **Toyo Suisan Kaisha, Tokyo** (est. 1953). US plants Irvine CA (since 1977), Richmond VA, Von Ormy TX; >3.6 billion packs/yr. No Canadian plant.
- In Canada: ramen packs via Walmart.ca; **Instant Lunch cups only patchy** (dollar stores/importers) — verify in-store before claiming availability.
- Verbatim — Chicken ramen (maruchan.com): Noodles: "…VEGETABLE OIL (CONTAINS ONE OR MORE OF THE FOLLOWING: CANOLA, COTTONSEED, PALM) **PRESERVED BY TBHQ**"; Soup: "SALT, SUGAR CONTAINS LESS THAN 1% OF: **MONOSODIUM GLUTAMATE**, MALTODEXTRIN…, HYDROLYZED CORN PROTEIN, HYDROLYZED WHEAT PROTEIN, HYDROLYZED SOY PROTEIN…"
- **Half-block trick: YES — "½ noodle block with ½ flavoring packet (43g)" = 760 mg (33% DV)** → ~1,520 mg per block.
- 25% Less Sodium Chicken variant: 570 mg/serving (still fried, still TBHQ).

## 9. DIASPORA-CHANNEL BIG SELLERS (brief tier)
- **Lucky Me! Pancit Canton** (Monde Nissin, Philippines, brand launched 1989) — at Save-On/Superstore. Antioxidant = "green tea extract"; MSG openly listed. Fried in palm oil.
- **MAMA Shrimp Tom Yum** (Thai President Foods, est. 1972) — Walmart.ca 60 g×5. Verbatim Canadian label incomplete — READ ON PACK, don't quote from research.
- **Paldo** (Korea Yakult lineage 1969, spun off 2012) — Walmart Canada carries Gomtang, Cheese Ramyun, etc.

## 10. WINNERS (label-verified, available in Canada)
- **WINNER 1 — Nongshim Shin Ramyun Light ("Shin Light"): "Air dried, not fried."** No palm oil in the noodle block; fat only from small seasoning-oil sachet (rice bran/canola/corn oils). "75% less fat" vs regular Shin (brand claim — attribute). Made in South Korea. At Walmart.ca (97 g family pack) and T&T (97g×4). **HONEST CAVEAT MANDATORY: sodium is NOT lower — ~1,800 mg per 97 g bag.** The win is only "not fried." Read that on camera.
- **WINNER 2 — Lotus Foods Millet & Brown Rice Ramen (with miso soup sachet): two-ingredient noodle block** — "*brown rice flour, *millet flour" (*organic); brand describes rice ramens as "never-fried." Sodium 590 mg per ½-pack (40 g) serving (~1,180 whole pack; near-zero if sachet skipped — the noodle itself has no added salt). At Real Canadian Superstore ("Lotus Millet & Brown Rice Ramen 80 g") + Well.ca. Note: also uses the ½-pack serving — say so for fairness.
- **WINNER 3 — Farkay Chinese Style Steam Fried Noodles, 397 g — the actually-Canadian-made one.** Trademark owner **Northwest Food Products Ltd., 351 Powell St, Vancouver, BC** (NOT Toronto — do not say Toronto). In every major chain (Metro, Superstore, Well.ca ~$5.46). Verbatim (Well.ca): "Flour (wheat flour, malted barley flour or amylase, niacin reduced iron thiamine mononitrate, riboflavin), water, liquid egg product (albumen, egg yolk…), palm oil, salt, annatto, cornstarch, baking powder, sodium bicarbonate, dimethyl/polysiloxane." Honest framing: "steam fried" still meets palm oil — its angle is **Canadian-made, contains actual egg, no seasoning sachet** (you season it yourself), not "not fried." Ties to the egg/noodle standard beat (Part 2, pending).
- Honourable mention only (patchy Canadian availability — Amazon.ca, not grocery): Mike's Mighty Good (steamed, not fried). Do NOT present as "available in Canada" at grocery. Also mainstream low-sodium picks: Maruchan 25% Less Sodium (570 mg), Mr. Noodles Less Sodium flat (Costco Business Centre).

## TBHQ SCORECARD (on-shelf Canada, verbatim evidence above)

- **No TBHQ (tocopherols / green tea extract / none):** Mr. Noodles, no name, Great Value, Compliments, Shin Ramyun, Shin Light, Buldak, Lucky Me!, Demae Ramen (307b/304), Lotus Foods, Farkay.

## MSG / HYDROLYZED SCORECARD
- **"Monosodium glutamate" spelled out:** Mr. Noodles, no name, Great Value, Compliments, Sapporo Ichiban, Maruchan, Lucky Me!, MAMA, Indomie (as "621").
- **No MSG-as-such but hydrolyzed proteins / yeast extract:** Nissin Cup Noodles US (with "No MSG" flash — the beat), Shin Ramyun US label, Buldak.
- Split: MSG/hydrolysates live in the **sachet**; the block carries oil, salt, alkaline carbonates, gums, antioxidant.
