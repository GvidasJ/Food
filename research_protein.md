## SECTION 2 — THE IOVATE FILE (CENTREPIECE — REVISED STORY ARC)

### 2.1 Founding: Oakville, Ontario
- MuscleTech began in **1995** as **MuscleTech Research and Development**, a direct-to-consumer mail-order business (per Wikipedia/Grokipedia summaries; MuscleTech infobox says "introduced 1995" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MuscleTech).
- **Paul Gardiner** is identified as founder: "Paul Gardiner founded Iovate Health Sciences in 1995" (ZoomInfo profile — https://www.zoominfo.com/p/Paul-Gardiner/2591793351 — **secondary source; verify against a primary bio before air**).
- Corporate history: "In 2009, Iovate Health Sciences Group, Inc. restructured and changed its name to Kerr Investment Holding Corp., a Canadian holding company" (Wikipedia, MuscleTech article).
- HQ: **381 North Service Road West, Oakville, Ontario** — confirmed on the federal CCAA record (below).
- Brand stable (from Iovate's own 2016 press release, next section): **MuscleTech, Six Star Pro Nutrition, Purely Inspired, Hydroxycut**.

### 2.2 The 2016 Chinese acquisition — verified verbatim
Bloomberg headline, June 13, 2016:
> "Xiwang Agrees to Acquire Canada's Iovate Health for $730 Million"
> — https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2016-06-13/xiwang-agrees-to-acquire-canada-s-iovate-health-for-730-million

**Currency caution:** the precise figure is **CDN$730 million**, not US$. Gowling WLG (Canadian counsel on the deal):
> "Xiwang Foodstuffs Company Ltd. ('Xiwang Food') announced that it had completed its previously announced acquisition of Iovate Health Sciences International, Inc. ('Iovate'), via its acquisition of Iovate's parent company, Kerr Investment Holding Corporation ('Kerr'), from The Toronto Oak Trust for a total consideration of CDN$730 million."
> — https://gowlingwlg.com/en/insights-resources/client-work/2016/gowling-wlg-provides-canadian-transactional-suppor

Wikipedia states the USD equivalent as "$584 million" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MuscleTech). Goodmans LLP (also deal counsel): "Iovate Health Sciences International Sells Majority Stake to Xiwang Foodstuffs Co. for $730 Million" — https://www.goodmans.ca/expertise/case/iovate-health-sciences-international-sells-majority-stake-to-xiwang-foodstuffs-co-for-730--million. Announced June 13, 2016; completed September 23, 2016 (Gowling, above).

Iovate's own press release (mirrored at Pharma Advancement; the iovate.com original returned 404 at time of research):
> "Iovate has reached a definitive agreement to receive a majority equity investment from Shenzhen-listed Xiwang Foodstuffs Co., Ltd."
> "Iovate will maintain its North American headquarters in Oakville, Canada. All Executives and Senior Leaders, as well as its 330 existing employees, will remain in place to take the business to the next level of growth."
> — https://www.pharmaadvancement.com/health-nutrition/sports-nutrition-leader-iovate-health-sciences-announces-strategic-investment-xiwang-foodstuffs-accelerate-growth-china/ (original URL: https://www.iovate.com/press-release/sports-nutrition-leader-iovate-health-sciences-announces-strategic-investment-by-xiwang-foodstuffs-to-accelerate-growth-in-china/)

Xiwang Food is based in Shandong Province, China (Bloomberg, above). So from Sept 2016: **the biggest sports-nutrition company headquartered in Canada was majority Chinese-owned.** That chain is fully verified — for the 2016–2025 period.

### 2.3 The 2025 collapse — public court record
Federal CCAA registry (Office of the Superintendent of Bankruptcy):
> Debtor companies: "XIWANG IOVATE HOLDINGS COMPANY LIMITED, IOVATE HEALTH SCIENCES INTERNATIONAL INC., IOVATE HEALTH SCIENCES U.S.A. INC., IOVATE HEALTH SCIENCES AUSTRALIA PTY LTD, and NORTHERN INNOVATIONS HOLDING CORP." — commencement "2025-10-31", Ontario Superior Court of Justice (Commercial List), court file "BK-25-03268936-0031", monitor "KSV RESTRUCTURING INC."
> — https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/office-superintendent-bankruptcy/en/ccaa-records-search/ccaa-records-xiwang-iovate-holdings-company-limited-et-al

Sequence (trade press + monitor documents):
- NOI filed under the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act **Sept 5, 2025**; continued under CCAA Oct 31, 2025 (NutraIngredients, Oct 2, 2025 — https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/10/02/iovate-health-sciences-makers-of-muscletech-and-hydroxycut-pursues-restructuring/; Insolvency Insider — https://insolvencyinsider.ca/p/iovate-health-s-noi-proceedings-continued-under-the-ccaa-as-walmart-freeze-and-orgain-enforcement-sq).
- Liquidity trigger per NutraIngredients (public court-record reporting, phrase as fact-of-record, not wrongdoing): a US court "awarded Orgain US$ 10,035,481" (April 2024), later "increased the judgement to US$ 12.5 million"; "Orgain obtained legal permission to garnish about US$ 8 million from Walmart, a major customer of Iovate. This money was vital for Iovate's cash flow, and the company subsequently defaulted on a major secured loan with the Royal Bank of Canada in June 2024." (URL above.)
- Iovate CEO Wes Parris, quoted in the same article: "This restructuring paves the way for us to execute on an aggressive growth strategy rooted heavily in product innovation..."

### 2.4 The 2026 sale — Xiwang wiped out, mystery buyer in
Arnold & Porter client advisory ("Time to Learn the 'Canadian Two-Step'?", May 2026):
> Purchaser: "1001542267 Ontario Inc. (the Purchaser), a newly formed entity."
> Equity outcome: "the Purchaser subscribed for and acquired 100 new common shares in the Purchased Company, while all existing shares were cancelled for no consideration."
> — https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/advisories/2026/05/time-to-learn-the-canadian-two-step

Translation for script (accurate): **Xiwang's equity in Iovate was cancelled for nothing.** Subscription agreement dated April 2, 2026; Canadian court approval April 16, 2026 (same source). Canaccord Genuity confirms closing:
> "Exclusive financial advisor to the Consortium on the acquisition and its associated debt financing" — closing **May 27, 2026**; "consortium of buyers" not named.
> — https://www.canaccordgenuity.com/capital-markets/transactions/2026/may/iovate/

Monitor's reports (KSV) are the primary record: https://www.ksvadvisory.com/docs/default-source/insolvency-case-documents/iovate/ccaa-proceedings/reports/iovate---fourth-report-of-the-monitor---final.pdf — a search summary of this report states that "as of April 2, 2026, the beneficial ownership of the Purchaser changed, and the Purchaser is no longer related to Iovate Holdings" (i.e., no longer related to the Xiwang side) — **read the PDF directly before air; the page verbatim was not extracted.** Industry rumour (MuscleInsider) named Fit Foods LP (MUTANT's parent) as a possible bidder — **rumour only, never aired as fact**: https://muscleinsider.com/news/mutant-buying-muscletech-iovate-sale-rumor/. US Chapter 15 recognition: https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/10857778/in-re-iovate-health-sciences-international-inc-et-al/.

**Consumer-facing point:** through all of this, Six Star and MuscleTech tubs on Walmart Canada shelves never changed. Ownership churned from a Toronto family trust to a Shandong conglomerate to an unnamed consortium — the label stayed the same.

## SECTION 3 — VEGA: THE BETRAYAL ARC (VERIFIED)

1. **Founding:** "Vega was founded in 2001 in Burnaby, Canada by Brendan Brazier and Charles Chang under the company name Sequel Natural Ltd." (Wikipedia — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vega_(company)); Chang launched Sequel Naturals in 2001 and collaborated with vegan endurance athlete Brazier from ~2003/2004 on the first Vega shake ("Whole Food Health Optimizer"). WhiteWave's own materials say Vega was "founded in 2004" (brand launch year) and "based in Vancouver, British Columbia."
2. **2015 — sold to WhiteWave:** Bloomberg: "WhiteWave Foods Plans to Acquire Vega for About $550 Million" (https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2015-06-10/whitewave-foods-to-buy-health-food-company-vega-for-550-million). McMillan LLP (deal counsel): "WhiteWave Foods acquires Vega for US$550 million" (https://mcmillan.ca/deals-cases/whitewave-foods-acquires-vega-for-us550-million/). WhiteWave completed the acquisition of Sequel Naturals Ltd. on August 1, 2015 for "approximately US$550 million in cash"; Vega net sales were then ~US$100M/yr (WhiteWave 10-K/8-K — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001555365/000155536516000038/wwav-20151231x10k.htm).
3. **2017 — Danone swallows WhiteWave** (Danone completed the WhiteWave acquisition in April 2017; referenced in Danone's 2021 Vega release and universally in deal coverage — https://www.just-food.com/news/danone-sells-vega-brand-to-us-private-equity-firm/).
4. **2021 — Danone dumps Vega to US private equity:** "In June 2021, WM Partners entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Vega from Danone, with the transaction completed in July 2021" (Wikipedia; BevNET: "Danone Sells Vega to WM Partners," terms not disclosed — https://www.bevnet.com/news/2021/danone-sells-vega-to-wm-partners/; WM Partners release — https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/wm-partners-announces-acquisition-of-vega-301343956.html; Danone PR PDF exists but was not machine-readable: https://www.danone.com/content/dam/corp/global/danonecom/investors/en-all-publications/2021/pressreleases/prDanone170621.pdf — pull manually for a Danone verbatim). Danone framed it as "part of its portfolio... continuous capital allocation optimization" (Food Business News — https://www.foodbusinessnews.net/articles/18895-private-equity-firm-to-acquire-danones-vega-brand). WM Partners bought via its "HPH II Investments Master Fund" (BevNET).
5. **Owner today:** WM Partners (Florida-based PE) — no subsequent sale found in searches through Sept 2026 (PitchBook profile current — https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/54433-45).
6. **The marketing vs. the cap table — Vega's own words, verbatim** (myvega.ca "Rooted in Canada" page — https://myvega.ca/pages/rooted-in-canada):
> "ROOTED IN CANADA, POWERED BY PLANTS"
> "Vega Canada was born in Vancouver, British Columbia, with a simple but powerful idea"
> "Founded by Canadian endurance athlete Brendan Brazier and nutrition expert Charles Chang, Vega pioneered a new way to fuel active lifestyles"
> "From the beginning of Vega, our Canadian roots run deep."
> "A majority of our Canadian products are manufactured in Canada."

That last line is the honest tell: **"a majority... manufactured in Canada"** — the company's own hedge. Where the rest is made is not stated. **UNVERIFIED: whether Vega still operates any Burnaby facility** — do not claim a closure on air; no closure coverage found.

## SECTION 4 — THE GLANBIA EMPIRE (IRELAND)

Glanbia plc: headquartered at "Glanbia House, Ring Road, Kilkenny" Ireland (GlobalData/company contact page — https://www.glanbia.com/contact); origins: "In 1964, the members of five Irish farmer co-operatives agree to join forces and become Waterford Co-operative Society Limited" (Glanbia "Our Journey" — https://www.glanbia.com/about/our-journey).

Acquisitions, verbatim from Glanbia's own timeline (same URL):
> **2008:** "a decision to vertically integrate with the acquisition of one of its customers - the US sports supplement firm Optimum Nutrition"
> **2011:** "In 2011, Glanbia purchases the US sports nutrition company BSN."
> **2014:** "In 2014 Nutramino and Isopure join the growing portfolio."
> **2015:** "2015 brings the addition of think!®, the Californian lifestyle nutrition brand"

Isopure price, from Glanbia's press release (Sept 12, 2014 — https://www.glanbia.com/media/press-releases/glanbia-acquires-isopure-us-sports-nutrition-business-us150-million):
> "Glanbia plc announces that it has agreed to acquire The Isopure Company, LLC for cash consideration of US$153 million."
(Headline says US$150M; body says US$153M — use the body figure.)

Scale (Glanbia 2024 Annual Report / FY2024 results — https://www.glanbia.com/media/press-releases/2024-full-year-results): GPN delivered revenue growth of 0.5% and EBITDA growth of 8.3% (constant currency) in 2024; **"Optimum Nutrition represents 66% of GPN revenue"**; ON grew 7.5% constant currency. Glanbia's marketing: ON Gold Standard is "the world's best-selling whey protein powder" (https://www.glanbia.com/news/optimum-nutrition-trusted-athletes-globally).

**The reveal line, supported:** the best-selling whey on Canadian shelves is the flagship brand of an Irish dairy company that started as five Irish farmer co-ops in 1964.

## 1. THE WEIDER BROTHERS — THE HERITAGE CLOSE (verified deeply)

**The payoff line holds up:** the modern supplement/bodybuilding industrial complex — the first sports nutrition company, the IFBB, Mr. Olympia, Muscle & Fitness, and the man who brought Arnold to America — was built by two brothers from Montreal.

### 1.1 Births — both born in Montreal
- Joe Weider: born **November 29, 1919, Montreal, Quebec, Canada** (Wikipedia — Joe Weider: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Weider)
- Ben Weider: born **February 1, 1923, Montreal, Quebec, Canada** (Wikipedia — Ben Weider: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Weider)

> "Born in Montreal to Polish parents, the boys grew up in a tough neighborhood, where Ben was frequently attacked by bullies."
> — Juifs d'ici (Federation CJA, Quebec Jewish community heritage site): https://www.juifsdici.ca/en/ben-joe-weider/

### 1.2 The barbell business from the family home (street address for B-roll)
> Joe Weider "built a set of barbells out of car wheels and axles the same year out of the family garage on **Coloniale Street in Montreal**."
> — Wikipedia — Joe Weider

The 1936 founding date is corroborated by a Canadian primary source — Concordia University's 1994 honorary doctorate citation for Ben Weider: "Founded in 1936 with his brother Joseph, Weider Sports Equipment Co. became 'the most important [global] distributor of physical conditioning equipment.' The Montreal-based company generated over $500 million in annual worldwide sales... It published four fitness magazines with 1.7 million monthly circulation and manufactured nearly 100 nutritional supplements." — https://www.concordia.ca/offices/archives/honorary-degree-recipients/1994/06/ben-weider.html

Wikipedia also states the family "founded Weider Nutrition in 1936, considered the first sports nutrition company." **Note for script accuracy:** use "the business Joe started in 1936" and "first sports nutrition company" with attribution flag.

### 1.3 The magazine — started 1940, from Montreal
> "In 1940, the brothers began publishing a bodybuilding magazine called *Your Physique*" — Juifs d'ici.
(The famous "$7 startup" figure is UNVERIFIED — see quarantine.)

### 1.4 IFBB founded in Montreal, 1946 — at a Mr. Canada contest
> "The IFBB was founded in 1946 in Montreal, Canada by brothers Joe Weider and Ben Weider."
> — IFBB official Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/International.Fitness.BodyBuilding.Federation/videos/1515502892709687/

Secondary sources (https://engravediniron.com/2017/12/09/the-international-federation-of-bodybuilding-origin/; https://www.ifbbpro.com/about-the-league/): in 1946 the Weiders rented **Montreal's Monument National Theatre** and hosted the first **Mr. Canada** contest; the IFBB was founded that night. Concordia citation: "In 1946, he established the International Federation of Bodybuilders, which grew to include 139 member nations." Ben Weider was IFBB president until October 29, 2006 (Wikipedia).

### 1.5 Mr. Olympia — created 1965
> The IFBB "created their first professional contest, the Mr. Olympia, which was held on September 18, 1965 at the Brooklyn Academy of Music in New York City." — https://www.ifbbpro.com/about-the-league/

### 1.6 Arnold — brought to America 1968; his own words
> "In 1968, the brothers brought Arnold Schwarzenegger to California." — Wikipedia — Joe Weider

Schwarzenegger's tribute on Joe's death (March 2013; NPR: https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2013/03/23/175148340/joe-weider-fitness-icon-and-mr-olympia-creator-dies-at-93; original: https://www.schwarzenegger.com/announcements/post/statement-on-the-passing-of-joe-weider):
> "I knew about Joe Weider long before I met him. He was the godfather of fitness who told all of us to 'Be Somebody with a Body.'"
> "Joe didn't just inspire my earliest dreams; he made them come true the day he invited me to move to America to pursue my bodybuilding career... he freely gave of his time and expertise and became a father figure for me."

The Hercules anecdote (AP-syndicated obituary via Legacy.com): Weider helped Arnold land his first movie role in "Hercules in New York" "by passing him off to the producers as a German Shakespearean actor." (https://www.legacy.com/legacy/notable-deaths/joe-weider)

### 1.7 The protein powders — among the first mass-market protein supplements
> "Crash Weight Gaining Formula #7 was the biggest selling product in the Weider Empire and the mainstay of the entire supplement line."
