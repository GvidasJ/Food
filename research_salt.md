# PART 1 — REGULATION, IODINE, INDUSTRY

## THE STANDARD — verbatim
**CFCS Volume 14 (the entire legal definition of salt is two sentences):**
> **"14.1.1 Salt**, other than crude rock salt, is crystalline sodium chloride and may contain not more than (a) 1.4%, singly or in combination, of calcium sulphate or potassium chloride; and (b) 0.1% of other substances naturally present in the salt."*

**FDR B.17.003 (current, verbatim) — THE SPINE:**
> *"(1) No person shall sell, for table or general household use, salt for which a standard is set out in Volume 14 of the Food Compositional Standards Document unless the salt contains **0.01% potassium iodide**. (2) The presence of iodide must be shown on the principal display panel."*

**B.17.004:** table salt *"may contain dextrose to stabilize potassium iodide"* — the sugar-in-salt decode, and it's in the regulation itself.

### The computation (mine, from the regulation — flag as computed)
0.01% KI = 100 mg KI/kg; KI is 76.45% iodine → **Canadian iodized salt carries ~76.5 µg iodine per gram — HIGHER than US iodized salt (~45 µg/g per NIH ODS).** Adult RDA (150 µg) ≈ **2 g of Canadian iodized salt, roughly ⅓ teaspoon.**

### Which salts must be iodized (CFIA Industry Labelling Tool — salt)
- **Table salt AND sea salt sold for table or general household use MUST be iodized.** CFIA verbatim: *"The addition of iodide to salt for table or general household use is required to help in the prevention of goiter."*
- **Pickling/coarse/specialty salt escapes** via the common name: *"A common name that indicates the use, such as 'salt for pickling' would be acceptable."*
- 🔥 **ONE LETTER CHANGES THE LAW:** *"The common name 'sea salt' may be used to refer to processed salt from an existing sea"* (standardized, iodization applies for table use) — but *"the common name **'sea salts' (note the plural)** refers to a mixture of sodium chloride and other mineral salts"* — a different, non-standardized product.
- Salt is **exempt from the Nutrition Facts table** (for declared iodide) and **"always exempt" from the front-of-package "High in sodium" symbol** — alongside butter, sugar, honey, oils (redundancy exemptions).
- **No specific rule exists for "fleur de sel" or "Himalayan" names**; salt has no mandatory country-of-origin labelling. (Say "no specific standard found," not "legally meaningless.")

### Additives (Health Canada List of Permitted Anticaking Agents)
- Calcium silicate, magnesium silicate, silicon dioxide etc. — ≤1.0% (2.0% fine-grained)
- **Sodium/potassium ferrocyanide — ≤13 ppm.**

### 🔥 The ferrocyanide decode (best label-scare/debunk beat)
"Yellow prussiate of soda" = sodium ferrocyanide (E535) — literally a cyanide compound — but the cyanide is locked to iron in a stable complex. **EFSA 2018 re-evaluation: ferrocyanides are "of no safety concern at the current authorised use and use levels"** (ADI 0.03 mg/kg bw/day). At 13 ppm, a 70-kg adult would need ~160 g of salt a day to reach the ADI — the sodium is lethal thousands of times over first. (ADI is EFSA's; the 160 g framing is computed.)

## IODINE — THE PUBLIC-HEALTH SPINE
- **The goiter belt:** the Great Lakes basin was its heart. US surveys, 1920s: goiter in **26–70% of children** across the region; Michigan 1924 survey **38.6% prevalence → 1.4% by 1951** after iodization. (⚠️ Canadian-city percentages could not be retrieved — use US Great Lakes figures, labelled as US.)
- **Canada made iodization of table salt MANDATORY in 1949** (peer-reviewed: AJCN; echoed by CBC 2025). **The US never did — American iodization is voluntary to this day.** First US iodized salt: Michigan, May 1, 1924.
- **DRIs (NIH ODS):** RDA 150 µg adults; **220 µg pregnancy, 290 lactation**; UL 1,100 µg.
- **Canadian status now — the quiet slide:**
  - StatCan CHMS 2009–2011: **22% of Canadians 3–79 at least mildly deficient**, 7% moderately.
  - CHMS Cycle 5 (2016-17, Bertinato, J Nutr): **women 20–39 at median ~100 µg/L urinary iodine — exactly the adequacy floor; ~32% of non-pregnant women 19–39 below the EAR.** Paper recommends women who could become pregnant take a multivitamin with iodine.
  - Nutrients 2022: 11.9% of adults below 50 µg/L (moderate-to-severe).
- **The mechanism (the video's argument):**
  1. **Food manufacturers "almost always use noniodized salt"** (NIH ODS verbatim) — so the 77% of sodium from processed food carries ~no iodine.
  2. Premium/sea/Himalayan/pickling salts escape iodization.
  3. Eating out more + cooking less = less iodized salt.
  - CBC 2025 framing: iodization is *"a public health victory… becoming a victim of its own success."*
- **WHO/UNICEF universal salt iodization since 1993**; 120+ countries; **<1¢/person/day, ~30:1 benefit-cost** — among the cheapest public-health wins in history.
- Thyroid one-liners (NIH ODS): iodine is an *"essential component of the thyroid hormones"*; goiter is *"the body's attempt to trap more iodine"*; severe deficiency in pregnancy → cretinism; *"mild to moderate iodine insufficiency during pregnancy may subtly affect fetal development."*

## SODIUM HONESTY (fairness — Health Canada's own report)
> *"**77% of dietary sodium comes from commercially processed and prepared foods, while only 11% is added by consumers**; the remaining 12% occurs naturally in foods."*
- Average 2,760 mg/day; 58% over the limit; 72% of kids 4–13.
- **Framing: the shaker is ~11% of the problem. This video is about WHICH salt, not MORE salt.**

## INDUSTRY CANADA — chronological
- **1893** — Windsor Salt founded (The Windsor Salt Company, by three CPR employees).
- **1949** — mandatory iodization.
- **1954** — Morton acquires Windsor Salt.
- **1959** — **Goderich ON mine opens — the world's largest underground salt mine, 1,800 ft under Lake Huron** ("as deep as the CN Tower is tall" — company's own claim). Compass Minerals since 1990.
- **2009** — Germany's K+S buys Morton (Windsor under German ownership).
- **Aug 21, 2011** — **F3 tornado (~320 km/h) comes off Lake Huron and hits the Sifto mine complex first**, then downtown Goderich. Sole fatality: miner **Normand Laberge, 61**, in a boom crane loading ships. $150M damage.
- **2018** — **12-week strike** at Goderich (Unifor Local 16-O, 341 workers; replacement workers inflamed it).
- **Oct 2020 → Apr 30, 2021** — **Stone Canyon Industries + Demetree buy Morton + Windsor from K+S for US$3.2 BILLION.** Windsor Salt is now American-owned.
- **Feb 17 – Aug 2023** — **Windsor Salt strike: ~189 days (~6 months)**, Unifor Locals 240 & 1959, Ojibway mine + evaporation plant; contracting-out was the core issue.
- **Apr 2024** — Compass lays off **87 Goderich miners (~22% of the mine's hourly workforce)** — the winter was too mild; road-salt stockpiles full.
- 🔥 **Oct–Dec 2024 — THE SALT RECALL CASCADE:** metal fragments in Sifto **Hy-Grade Salt** from the Goderich plant (Class II, salt made Sept 18–Nov 6 2024) → **Nov 4: Wonder Brands recalls 37 bread/bun products** (Wonder, D'Italiano, Country Harvest, Great Value, No Name) → then Griffith dry blends, **McCain potato products**, bagels, pita, sausages, **butter** through Dec 13 2024. **No injuries.** One ingredient, half the bakery aisle.
- **Scale:** Canada produced ~**16 Mt of salt in 2023**, top-five in the world — overwhelmingly **road salt**. Canadians are the world's largest per-capita salt consumers (roads, not food).
- **Pink salt reality check (peer-reviewed, *Foods* 2020, Australia):** of pink salts retailed, one sample (Peru) exceeded the lead limit; **you'd need >30 g/day (~6 tsp) of pink salt for any meaningful mineral contribution** — far past safe sodium. ⚠️ This is the citable pink-salt study. Do NOT import the Mamavation/Lead Safe Mama ppb tables.

## STRONGEST REVEALS — ranked
1. **The $2 box is the fortified one.** Illegal since 1949 to sell non-iodized table salt in Canada (FDR B.17.003, 0.01% KI). The US never made it mandatory. Canada's level (~76 µg/g) beats America's (~45).
2. **The premium-salt loophole** — name your salt for a different use and the iodine mandate vanishes. Upgrade your shaker, zero your iodine.
3. **Processed-food salt is not iodized** (NIH ODS verbatim) — 77% of Canadian sodium carries no iodine. Sodium up, iodine down.
4. **The slide is measurable** — women of childbearing age at the adequacy floor; ~32% below the EAR; 1 in 5 Canadians at least mildly deficient.
5. **One letter changes the law** — "sea salt" vs "sea salts."
6. **"Yellow prussiate of soda" is a cyanide compound, capped at 13 ppm — and EFSA says no safety concern.** Scare decoded honestly.
7. **The 2024 recall cascade** — one Goderich salt product → 37 breads → McCain fries → butter.
8. **The entire legal definition of salt is two sentences** — and salt is exempt from the Nutrition Facts table and the new magnifying-glass symbol.
9. **The world's largest underground salt mine is Canadian** — CN-Tower-deep under Lake Huron; tornado 2011 (hit the mine first, killed a miner); strike 2018; 87 laid off in 2024 because winter was too mild.
10. **Iodization is history's cheapest public-health win** — <1¢/day, ~30:1; Michigan goiter 38.6% → 1.4%. Canada locked it in law; the US left it to the market.
