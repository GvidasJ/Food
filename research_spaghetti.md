## Decodes
**a. NO legal durum requirement in Canada.** The only FDR "alimentary paste" provisions: B.13.051 (egg pasta ≥4% egg-yolk solids) and B.13.052 (enrichment table). **No compositional standard of identity for spaghetti exists** — CFCS Vol. 12 covers flour and bread, not pasta. Legally Canadian "spaghetti" could be any wheat flour. **The punchline: nobody exploits it — the market is durum anyway.** (Italy requires 100% durum by law.)

**b. Enrichment is VOLUNTARY in Canada** (B.13.052(2): "enriched" requires thiamine, riboflavin, niacin, folic acid AND iron within table ranges — iron 2.90–4.30 mg/100 g). CFIA confirms permitted-not-required. 🔥 **The label tell: the iron %DV line.** Enriched ≈ 15–20%+ DV; unenriched ≈ 3–8%. no name = unenriched; Italpasta = 19%.

**c. The Catelli "All-Natural" paradox** — enrichment traded away for clean-label positioning (if the box confirms the site). Either way it demonstrates (b) on camera.

**d. Protein tell — the honest Canadian version.** Canadian NFts use ~85 g dry (US uses 56 g — **never compare across borders without converting**). Canadian shelf: 10–11 g/85 g nearly everywhere; premium imports ≈ 11–12 g/85 g (De Cecco 14 g/100 g). **The chasm the US video描述 is a ~1–2 g gap here.**

**e. Origin claims:** Primo/Unico print "Canadian durum" **in the ingredient line itself** — stronger than a logo. Barilla blue box = US-made. Kirkland organic = Garofalo Italy ⚠️ pack-verify.

**f. Bronze-cut/slow-dried:** no legal definition — visible matte surface is the only check.

**g. "No preservatives" on dry pasta is a nothing-claim** — 12% moisture needs none; nobody's contains any.

# PART 2 — DURUM TRADE & REGULATION (agent dossier, 2026-08-23)

## a. Canada = durum superpower (all government-sourced)
- **2025-26 production: 7.1 Mt** — up 12% y/y, "largest since 2016-2017" (AAFC Dec 2025 outlook). **Saskatchewan grows 76%, Alberta 22%** — 98.6% of all Canadian durum is planted in Sask+Alta (USDA GAIN citing StatCan).
- **World's #1 durum exporter** (USDA FAS verbatim: "Canada is the world's leading durum exporter"). MY 2024/25 exports: **5,798,615 t, up 65% y/y**. AAFC/IGC math: Canada ≈ **62% of world durum trade** (Reuters has said "about two-thirds"). On air: "roughly six of every ten tonnes of durum traded on Earth."
- **Italy is Canada's LARGEST durum export market** (Cereals Canada: avg 927,000 t / **$421M per year**, 2020–24). Honest cap: Italy imports ~30% of the wheat its pasta industry needs; Canada is usually its #1–2 foreign supplier — do NOT say "most Italian pasta is Canadian wheat."
- Why Italian mills buy it: No. 1 CWAD grade spec = **min 80% hard vitreous ("glassy") kernels, 79 kg/hL, 9.5% protein floor** (CGC Official Grain Grading Guide); 2025 crop averaged **14.6% protein**. Prized for gluten strength, bright yellow colour, cooking quality.

## b. The Italy fight — "grano canadese" timeline
- 2015: Italy's farm lobby **Coldiretti** starts campaigning against Canadian durum over pre-harvest glyphosate. 2016: Italy bans pre-harvest glyphosate domestically.
- **June 2017 — the Bari incident:** a ~50,000-tonne shipload of Canadian durum seized at the port of Bari on a toxicity claim; certified re-test found levels **within safety limits**; ship released — after "contaminated wheat" headlines had run. Italy's own millers' association (Italmopa) called it a "demonization" campaign. ⚠️ CBC article blocked to fetch — verify manually before quoting verbatim.
- **Aug 2017:** Italy's country-of-origin decree — pasta labels must name where the wheat grew. EU Commission opposed it as a trade barrier. "Introduced 2017 and repeatedly extended" (verified only to end-2022 — never say "still in force").
- **The collapse:** Canadian durum to Italy fell from **1.4 Mt (2020/21) to 0.3 Mt (2021/22)** (USDA GAIN table — safest on-air series). **Barilla cut Canadian purchases 35%** and set a private spec of **10 parts per BILLION glyphosate — 500x stricter than Canada's legal limit** — while calling Canadian durum "of exceptional quality" (Reuters).
- **La Molisana's "100% grano italiano"** = built in this exact fight. The bag in a Canadian store is a trade-war artifact aimed at Canadian wheat.
- **The recovery:** Italy quietly came back — 1.16 Mt (2022/23), 0.90 Mt (2024/25) — while the flags stayed on the bags. Irony: 2020 Italian consumer-lab tests found glyphosate traces in **seven famous Italian brands** — all within EU limits (ilsalvagente.it).
- **House-style science framing (mandatory):** Health Canada PMRA 2017 re-evaluation: no risks of concern when used per label. **Canada's legal glyphosate limit for wheat (5 ppm) is HALF the EU's (10 ppm)** and 1/6 the US's (30 ppm). CFIA tested 3,188 foods: 98.7% compliant, no health risk found even in exceedances. EFSA 2023: no critical areas of concern; EU re-approved glyphosate to 2033. → This is a TRADE story, not a safety story.

## c. Regulation decode (verified verbatim, post-SOR/2024-244)
- **B.13.051 verbatim** (egg pasta): "...as egg macaroni, egg spaghetti, egg noodles... unless they contain, on the dry basis, not less than four per cent, egg-yolk solids..."
- **B.13.052(2) verbatim:** "No person shall represent an alimentary paste as 'enriched' unless the alimentary paste contains added thiamine, riboflavin, niacin, folic acid and iron, in accordance with the table to this section." Iron range 2.90–4.30 mg/100 g (→ the %DV tell). CFIA confirms: pasta "**can** be enriched" = VOLUNTARY.
- Machine-searched the full CFCS: "spaghetti," "alimentary paste," "semolina," "durum" appear NOWHERE. **No compositional standard for pasta exists in Canada.** Italy requires durum by law; the US has a macaroni standard (21 CFR 139); Canada has nothing.
- 🔥 **The folic-acid paradox:** unenriched WHITE FLOUR is illegal in Canada (B.13.001 — every loaf of bread is fortified by law), but durum semolina is not "white flour," so pasta can legally carry **zero added folic acid**. The vitamins Ottawa decided your bread must have are optional in your spaghetti. (no name = exactly this: sole ingredient, unenriched.)

## d. Prices & the 2021 shock
- StatCan 18-10-0245-01 (dry pasta 500 g): Jan 2017 **$2.36** → Jan 2021 $2.50 → **peak Dec 2022 $3.64 (+46% in 21 months)** → June 2026 **$3.39**. Never came back down.
- **2021 drought:** durum production cut ~50% below 2020; export prices nearly doubled to 13-year highs; Alberta durum hit ~$500/t (+70%); budget pasta in FRANCE rose ~20% — a Saskatchewan drought moved pasta prices in Paris. Current: No. 1 CWAD ~$280/t (AAFC 2025-26), down from $425 in 2023-24 — **wheat price halved; the bag didn't follow.** (~500 g bag contains ≈ 35–40¢ of durum at peak, ~14¢ now — do the math on air carefully: $280/t = 28¢/kg = **~14¢ of wheat in a $3.39 bag**.)
