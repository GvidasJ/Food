### A5. ⚠️ THE FAIRNESS FRAME — NON-NEGOTIABLE SCRIPT GUIDANCE

### B2. Documented change to the blend or supplier

**This is the single most-repeated audience claim and it is only half true. Get it right on air.**

The claim, as Canadians put it **(d)**: Tim Hortons dropped Mother Parkers, McDonald's Canada picked up the old beans, and McCafé now tastes like old Tim's.

What is actually supported **(b)**:
- Mother Parkers **did** supply Tim Hortons before Tim Hortons brought roasting in-house; Mother Parkers **does** supply McDonald's Canada. Multiple secondary sources agree. ⚠️ I did not find a tier-(a) primary confirmation of either leg. **Flag as "widely reported" or leave out.**
- Tim Hortons in **2019 publicly denied** selling its recipe, blend or beans to McDonald's, and describes the roast profile as proprietary. Reported by Yahoo Finance Canada — https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/tim-hortons-did-not-sell-coffee-recipe-to-mc-donalds-194810309.html. **Attribute as a corporate denial.**
- The company's own current position **(a)**: "the same top-secret recipe since 1964" (news.timhortons.ca); "Only 3 people know the secret recipe" (GCC).

**Script line that is safe and accurate:**
> "The company says the Original Blend recipe has not changed since 1964. What did change is who roasts it — Tim Hortons brought roasting in-house, and its former supplier now supplies McDonald's Canada. Tim Hortons has publicly denied ever handing its recipe or beans to a competitor. Both things can be true, and that's probably why the story won't die."

⚠️ **Note for the record:** the 2009 Ancaster start date and the loss of the third-party supplier relationship happen in the same window. Do **not** assert causation on air without a primary document.
