## The two lists (the core decode, label-proof)
- **Re-treated municipal ("tap, filtered hard"):** Dasani (Brampton/Calgary), Aquafina (Mississauga/Vancouver), Smartwater (undisclosed input). The label ALWAYS confesses: "demineralized," "remineralized," "distilled." Never the word "spring."
- **Actual named-source spring:** Pure Life (Hope BC), Eska, Naya, Montclair, Ice River, Flow, Evian, Fiji + every store brand (Kirkland/GV/PC/Compliments/Selection all say "spring water" — sources unnamed).
- 🔥 **The punchline: the CHEAPEST water in the store (Kirkland 11¢/bottle, GV 12¢) is real spring water, while Dasani/Aquafina/Smartwater at 6–30x the price are re-treated city tap.** Mirrors the pasta/no name reveal — the budget bottom shelf passes; the mid-shelf giants don't.

## Verbatim label quotes (read on camera)
- Aquafina (Superstore product name): **"AQUAFINA Demineralized Treated Water"**
- Dasani: **"Remineralized Water"** — ingredients: **"Filtered demineralized water, magnesium sulphate, potassium chloride, salt, ozone"** (magnesium sulphate = Epsom salt; potassium chloride = salt substitute; salt = salt)
- Smartwater: **"Vapour distilled water, calcium chloride, magnesium chloride, potassium bicarbonate, ozone"** — "electrolytes added for taste"; process "inspired by the clouds"
- Pure Life site: "sourced from carefully selected and monitored springs" — © **"Triton Water Canada Holdings, Inc."** (the tell: not Nestlé)
- Naya: "never treated with ozone… naturally sodium-free"
- Aquafina 2007 (attributed to coverage): label said "P.W.S." — after Corporate Accountability International pressure, PepsiCo agreed July 2007 to print "Public Water Source." Pepsi spokesperson: "If this helps clarify the fact that the water originates from public sources, then it's a reasonable thing to do."

## "Added minerals for taste" decode
Dasani adds magnesium sulphate (sold in pharmacies as Epsom salt), potassium chloride (fertilizer/salt-substitute compound), plain salt. Smartwater adds calcium chloride, magnesium chloride, potassium bicarbonate. Trace amounts, legal, disclosed, safe — labels say "for taste" because fully demineralized water tastes flat. HOUSE FRAMING: the decode is price-vs-source, NOT safety. No "chemicals in your water" framing.

## Ownership stories
1. 🔥 **"Nestlé" Pure Life hasn't been Nestlé since 2021.** Nestlé Waters NA → One Rock Capital/Metropoulos US$4.3B (spring 2021) → BlueTriton → merged with Primo Water Nov 8 2024 = **Primo Brands**, US company. The Canadian site's copyright line reads "Triton Water Canada Holdings."
2. 🔥 **Aberfoyle: the most famous water plant in Canada stopped bottling Jan 31, 2025** (announced Nov 13 2024, 144 layoffs). The Puslinch property + Aberfoyle/Hillsburgh water-taking permits went to **White Wolf Property Management — owned by James and Alexandra Gott of Ice River Springs** — reported ~$61M (GuelphToday land-registry reporting; Wellington Advertiser: price not disclosed). Permit transfer used a streamlined process with **no public consultation** (criticism attributed to Wellington Water Watchers; Puslinch mayor disputes "win" framing). Note: regulators effectively killed the first Nestlé→Ice River sale in 2020 — the same family got the wells five years later anyway, via a property company.
3. 🔥 **Eska — "Quebec's pristine esker water" — is owned by Morgan Stanley** (Morgan Stanley Strategic Investments; $18M asset purchase out of insolvency, Aug 2005). NOT "Chinese-owned" (2011 export deal only — do not conflate).
4. ✅ **Naya went the OTHER way: back to 100% Québec ownership March 2021** (Champlain Financial + GefCo bought from US PE firm L Catterton). The reverse-Catelli story — a winner beat.
5. **Flow: receivership Sept 4, 2025, ~$71M owed**, three failed sale attempts; July 2026 CFIA mould recall (flavoured line, Class 3). The "premium alkaline" business model failed.
6. **Montclair** — Canada's oldest water brand (founded by Canadiens owner Léo Dandurand) — now US-owned (Primo), its Ontario plant closed.
7. ✅ **Ice River Springs (winner):** Gott family, Feversham ON, 1995. Built North America's first beverage-company-owned blue-box recycling operation (Blue Mountain Plastics, Shelburne ON, ~29,000 t/yr) — bottles are 100% recycled plastic, they make their own resin. Canada's biggest private-label water bottler (which store brands exactly = unverified, don't name). And the family now holds the old Nestlé wells.

## Deposits (brief beat)
**Ontario + Manitoba = only provinces with NO deposit on water bottles** (Ontario confirmed 2024 it won't add one — blue box only). BC 10¢; AB 10¢/25¢; **Quebec added 10¢ on water bottles Mar 1, 2025**; Maritimes/NL long-standing. Ontario is where most bottling happens — and the only place the bottle is worth nothing back.
