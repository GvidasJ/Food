## The playbook being replicated (what CE actually does)
1. ONE universal staple per video — never niche items. Their flops are the niche picks (baking soda 3K, almonds 16K, hamburger buns 12K); their monsters are daily staples (bread 3.29M, ice cream 950K, tuna 904K, pasta sauce 863K, butter 742K, bacon 683K).
2. "N to avoid (And N That Are Actually Real)" — GENEROUS redemption lists (8 that do, 11 worth it). Half warning, half buying guide.
3. Long runtime (25–30 min), consistent title formula, consistent thumbnail language.
4. High cadence — daily for CE; for CC the takeaway is consistency + topic quality, not literally daily.
5. Ride topic clusters while hot (their ground beef dropped into an existing 5-channel wave).
6. Note: Food Fraud Watch ran the same formula WITH heavy fear framing and flatlined; CE's lighter touch is winning. CC's no-fear rule is an advantage, not a constraint.
