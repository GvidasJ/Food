On September 24th, 2026, Costco told its shareholders it took in 5.9 billion U.S. dollars in membership-fee revenue across all its countries in the fiscal year that had just ended. Costco does not say how much of it came from Canada. The same filing counts 115 Costco warehouses in Canada, and Costco's Canadian website lists four more opening in November, in northeast Edmonton, Lloydminster, east Windsor and Wasaga Beach. Costco's last annual report put its Canadian business at 36.9 billion U.S. dollars in revenue for fiscal 2025, with about 1.85 billion U.S. dollars in operating income and 55,000 employees. If your card renews this fall, your 65 or 130 dollars is part of next year's number. So before it charges again, we read the rules you agree to when you pay. That means Costco Canada's membership terms, the version dated August 1st, 2026, line by line, plus the bank's terms for the card that doubles as your membership card. Ten things in them every member should know before renewing, in the order your membership year happens. Then the arithmetic on who should renew, and who shouldn't. Because the truth is not always on the menu.

The order is simple. The renewal charge, the upgrade, what counts toward the reward, when it's counted, how you pay, what happens after you buy, and what happens if you want out. Then, last, Costco's own numbers on who renews. A note first. In May we made a video with this same title. Costco's terms are now dated August 1st, 2026, and separately, some of what we said in May we can't stand behind. The price-tag codes, the lists of who makes Kirkland products, our gas-price gap and our Canadian member count came from sources we wouldn't use today. And we said the Executive reward counts at the gas pump. Costco's terms say it doesn't. This video replaces that one. We have no commercial relationship with Costco, CIBC or any company named in this video.

Number one, the renewal charge. Costco's terms say the Gold Star membership is 65 dollars, plus applicable taxes, per 12-month period from the date the primary cardholder enrolled. The Executive membership is 130 dollars, plus applicable taxes. Here is how the charge actually lands. If you have not signed up for auto renewal, the terms say your fee will be charged on your first shop of your renewal month. If you have, it goes on a Mastercard credit card or a Visa credit or debit card on the first day of your renewal month. Renew within two months after your membership expires, and the new year runs from the old expiry date. Renew later than that, and it runs from the day you pay. And the terms say all renewals will be at the membership fee in effect on the date the fee is paid. The last change was September 1st, 2024, when Costco's own filing says the fees went from 60 to 65 dollars and from 120 to 130. Before that, its filings show increases including 2006, 2011 for Canadian Business and Executive members, and June 2017. Membership is available to qualifying individuals 16 or older, and Canadian government ID must be shown at the counter when you apply. The fee also comes with rules about the card. One free household card goes to someone over 16 living at the same address, and Costco asks for proof of that address. Costco's terms say you will be required to scan your card when entering any Costco warehouse and at the payment register. The physical card or the card in the Costco app both work, but bar codes, photos or other copies are not acceptable. CBC reported in August 2024 that entrance scanners were already set up in Ottawa, Edmonton, Regina and B.C.'s Lower Mainland. Global News reported on September 25th, 2026 that the fees have not changed since 2024. So find your renewal month in your account before the bill finds you. The month matters more than the day.

Number two, the Executive upgrade. Costco's join page says the upgrade costs an additional 65 dollars a year, plus sales tax where applicable, prorated on the months left in your membership. It also says purchases made before you upgrade do not count. Then comes a sentence Costco puts in writing. The reward is not guaranteed to be equal to or greater than the Executive upgrade fee paid. That's Costco's own page. Here is the arithmetic. Costco calls the reward approximately 2 percent. Sixty-five dollars divided by 2 percent is 3,250 dollars of qualifying pre-tax purchases a year to break even, before any sales tax on the fee. That's about 271 dollars a month. Executive members in Canada do get one more thing in writing. Since June 30th, 2025, Costco's page offers a 10 dollar monthly Instacart credit on one order of 150 dollars or more of eligible products, and says the offer can be changed or cancelled at any time. What about early shopping hours? Costco's 2025 annual report describes exclusive Executive hours in the U.S. The three Canadian warehouse pages we checked, in Ontario, B.C. and Quebec, list one schedule for everyone, so check your own warehouse's page. Costco's pages also list Costco Services discounts, online wills and the Costco Connection magazine by mail for Executive members. And its terms allow one Executive membership per business or household. You can upgrade at a membership counter, by phone, or in your costco.ca account, where the prorated fee shows before you pay. The 2 percent may not cover the 65 dollars, and Costco says so first.

Number three, what the 2 percent does not count. This is the list to read before you do any math. Costco's terms say rewards will not be calculated on tobacco, or on purchases not recorded through the front-end registers, such as services, purchases at Costco's gas stations, food courts, optical centres in Quebec, and pharmacies. They also exclude membership fees, miscellaneous fees, deposits and taxes, services including auto, and certain other categories as determined from time to time at Costco's sole discretion. Costco also publishes a separate exclusions list. It adds Costco Shop Cards, gift certificates, tire mounting and balancing fees, and in Quebec and Nova Scotia, liquid milk and cream. In Quebec, the list also excludes all pharmacy and optical centre items. In May, we told you the Executive 2 percent stacks at the gas bar. Costco's terms say rewards are not calculated on purchases at its gas stations. If you fill up at Costco, none of that gas counts toward the reward. Some things do count. Costco's terms say the reward covers purchases by Canadian residents through the front-end registers at Costco warehouses in Canada and the United States, and online at costco.ca. Costco Travel counts too, on qualified purchases made directly from Costco Travel, applied after the trip, as long as you're an Executive member when the travel starts. Same-Day orders through Instacart are not on that list, so don't count them unless your reward shows them. Only purchases by the primary and household cardholders count. The concession is that the list is published, in plain words, on Costco's own page. So before you do any math, take everything on that list off the top, starting with gas and the food court.

Number four, the cap, the cut-off and the coupon. Costco's terms say the calculation of a reward is capped at, and will not exceed, 1,250 dollars for any 12-month period. You would need 62,500 dollars of qualifying purchases to hit it. That cap has moved over the years, from 500 dollars to 750 in 2011, to 1,000 in 2017, and to 1,250 in 2024, according to Costco's filings. Across all its countries, Costco's 2025 annual report counts the 2 percent rewards at about 3 billion U.S. dollars for the year. The reward period does not run the full year either. The terms say it runs from your enrollment or upgrade to about three months before your renewal date, and purchases from the last three months are added to the following year's calculation. So those purchases count toward next year's reward instead, if you're still an Executive member. The coupon is mailed about two months before your membership expires, along with the renewal notice. Only the primary member can redeem it, and only at Costco warehouses in Canada. It can't be used on alcohol, tobacco, travel or other services, or at the gas stations, food courts, pharmacies, or online at costco.ca. Costco's page says reward coupons will not be replaced if lost or stolen. And here is the line that matters at renewal. Executive members who downgrade, or cancel and receive a refund of their fees, will not receive a reward. So if you're thinking of downgrading or cancelling for a refund, count the reward you'd give up first. The terms also say Costco reserves the right, at its discretion, to discontinue or change the reward program at any time. The coupon arrives with the renewal notice.

Number five, the credit card is a separate deal. Since March 4th, 2022, the Costco Mastercard in Canada has been issued by CIBC. Before that it was Capital One, and The Canadian Press reported in 2014 that Costco Canada would stop taking American Express on December 31st that year. CIBC's page says the card has a zero dollar annual fee, with up to three additional cardholders at no cost, and that it is also your Costco membership card. The rates in the fine print on Costco's site are 3 percent at Costco gas in Canada and 2 percent at other gas stations and electric vehicle charging, on the first 5,000 dollars a year combined, then 1 percent. It's 2 percent on costco.ca on the first 8,000 dollars, then 1 percent. It's 3 percent at restaurants, and 1 percent on everything else, including in the warehouse. The 5,000 and 8,000 dollar limits reset every January 1st. The cash back comes as a certificate once a year, in January. CIBC's page lists a minimum annual income of 15,000 dollars for the basic card. There is also a new-member offer on Costco's site, a 65 dollar statement credit if you join as a new Executive member, apply for the card the same day, and spend 65 dollars on it within 120 days. It only applies to new members, or to memberships expired for more than 18 months, and upgrades don't qualify. Costco's customer service page says its Canadian warehouses take Mastercard, debit, cash, Costco Shop Cards, preprinted personal cheques with conditions, and Apple Pay, and that costco.ca also takes Visa. And the gas station is open to members only, Costco's page says, with one exception, Costco Shop Card holders. In May, we put an 8,000 dollar cap on the personal card's restaurant rate. In the fine print on Costco's site, that cap is in the Business card's terms. On the personal card, 8,000 dollars is the costco.ca limit. So you now have two rewards, on two calendars, with two sets of exclusions. The Executive reward follows your membership year, and the card follows January to December. The concession is real, there's no annual fee. But it's a credit card, so read its interest rate before you use it to chase cash back.

Number six, price drops, and the price you see online. Costco's customer service page says it will honour price adjustment requests for purchases made in the warehouse within 30 days of the purchase date. The item must be in stock, not a demo model, and within the valid promotional dates. If it qualifies, you ask at the membership counter and get the difference. So keep your receipt, and check prices within 30 days. Online orders have their own policy, and online prices are a separate question. Costco.ca product pages say standard shipping is included in the quoted price, that small grocery orders carry a surcharge, and that warehouse pricing may vary. Costco's page says products sold online may have different pricing than the same products at your local warehouse, because of shipping and handling, and that costco.ca does not price match the warehouse, or the other way around. And its Same-Day help page says item prices are marked up higher than your local warehouse, and that Instacart uses the markup to pay for delivery. The Same-Day site adds a 35 dollar order minimum, and says Instacart Plus members get lower item pricing than everyone else. Online pricing is also the subject of a proposed class action in Federal Court, file T-3644-24, filed in December 2024. It is an allegation, no court has ruled on it, it has not been certified, and Costco has moved to strike it. The court has indicated a certification hearing window of December 2026 to February 2027. Thirty days, in stock, in the promotion window.

Number seven, returns. Costco's terms say, we guarantee your satisfaction on every product we sell, and will refund your purchase price, with exceptions. The exceptions are in writing. Electronics, including televisions, major appliances, computers, tablets, cameras, drones and phones, have 90 days. A diamond of one carat or more needs its original certificates and about two to five business days to verify. Cigarettes and alcohol can't come back where the law prohibits it. Tires and batteries may come with their own limited warranty. Custom-made products can't be returned, except under warranty or where the law requires, and neither can gold or silver bullion, e-certificates, gift cards or tickets. Costco's customer service version of the policy adds airline and live event items, and Costco Shop Cards. In Quebec, the terms carry a notice that Costco does not guarantee the availability of replacement parts or repair services. The terms also say Costco may restrict its return policy in the future, and that restrictions will be shown at the point of purchase. One more line in the same document says Costco may end a membership without cause, or for abuse of membership privileges. The terms set no general time limit, and the exceptions are written down. Check for a restriction at the point of purchase, before you buy.

Three left, and the last one is the number Costco itself reports. If you'd rather have the fine print read for you before the next bill lands, take a second to subscribe. This channel reads the documents, so you don't have to.

Number eight, what's bundled in. Costco's Technical and Warranty Service page says that when the manufacturer's warranty is shorter, Costco extends it to two years from purchase on televisions, projectors, major appliances and computers, not tablets. It comes with free phone support in English and French. In May we called that service Concierge. Costco's page calls it Costco Technical and Warranty Service. Tires come bundled too. Costco's tire page says installation is included at no charge, along with a five-year road hazard warranty, rotation and balancing, flat repairs and nitrogen inflation, though the page says the warranty isn't available for all vehicles and tires. The same page also says additional component costs, including tire pressure sensor service fees, may apply. When we checked on October 1st, Costco's tire page listed 100 dollars off four or more Michelin passenger or light truck tires and 60 dollars off a set of four BFGoodrich, from September 28th to November 1st, 2026, conditions apply. And one date for your calendar. The Canadian warehouse pages we checked list Thanksgiving, Monday October 12th, as closed. Bundled isn't the same as free. Read the line that says what costs extra.

Number nine, the membership refund itself. Costco says it three slightly different ways, and you should know which page you're reading. The terms say, we will cancel and refund your membership fee at any time if you are dissatisfied. The join page says, we will cancel and refund your membership at any time if you are dissatisfied. A customer service page says it will refund your membership fee in full if you are dissatisfied. To cancel, the primary member goes to a membership counter. Remember number four, though. An Executive member who cancels and takes the refund gives up that year's reward. In May we said the reward is deducted from the refund. The terms say you simply don't receive it. And remember number seven. The terms also let Costco end a membership at its discretion, without cause, or for abuse of membership privileges, so we won't coach a refund play. One more rule, from the top of the same terms. Except for B.C. residents, the terms say Costco can amend its conditions without prior written notice or consent, but the changes apply upon the renewal of your membership. In B.C., changes come with prior notice, and they can't cut Costco's refund obligations. This video reads the version dated August 1st, 2026. It's a written guarantee, and it's the way out if your own receipts say the fee isn't worth it. The guarantee is how you test the fee, not how you game it.

Number ten, Costco's own numbers on who renews. Costco's September 24th, 2026 release gives a renewal rate of 92.3 percent for the U.S. and Canada together. Costco does not publish a Canada-only rate, so don't let anyone tell you that number is Canadian. Worldwide, it was 89.8 percent. Costco counted 84.1 million paid memberships worldwide, and 42.3 million of them Executive. It said Executive members made 75.6 percent of its worldwide sales. And here's a Canadian number that is labelled Canadian. In Costco's fiscal fourth quarter, the 16 weeks to August 30th, 2026, its comparable sales in Canada, excluding gas and exchange rates, were up 4.6 percent, against 7.2 percent in the U.S. By Costco's own filings, the U.S. and Canada rate has stayed around 90 percent for more than a decade, from 89.7 percent in fiscal 2012. So in the U.S. and Canada together, about nine in ten memberships renew. This video is about whether yours should.

So here's the arithmetic, with Costco's numbers and yours. Start with the Gold Star fee, 65 dollars plus tax. Whether it pays depends on what you actually buy. For example, if Costco saves you about 10 percent on what you buy there, you need roughly 650 dollars a year of those purchases, plus tax on the fee, to cover Gold Star. We compared 15 everyday items on retailers' own websites on October 1st, 2026. Costco Same-Day, Real Canadian Superstore, No Frills and Walmart were set to Mississauga, while costco.ca, Voilà and Giant Tiger showed online or Ontario prices. These were costco.ca prices, which include shipping, and Same-Day prices, which Costco says are marked up above the warehouse. Costco doesn't publish shelf prices, so these may not match what you'd pay in a warehouse. Costco was cheaper per unit on some bulk national brands and some premium Kirkland items. Terra Delyssa extra virgin olive oil, 3 litres on costco.ca, was 36 to 44 percent less per 100 millilitres than Terra Delyssa 1 litre bottles on Walmart, No Frills and Superstore's sites. Free-run eggs were 15 to 29 percent less than other free-run eggs. Tide was 6 to 13 percent less per load, and Kirkland Dark Colombian ground coffee was 12 to 31 percent less per 100 grams than other Colombian ground coffees. It was not cheaper on 2 percent milk or store-brand butter that day. Its solid light tuna and Thai jasmine rice cost more than other grocers' chunk tuna and other rice brands. Chicken breast cost more than at Walmart and a No Frills special, and Same-Day had no regular large eggs to compare. Other Costco wins, on oats and paper goods, were promotions ending in October, and some of the other stores' lower prices were specials ending October 7th. Now the upgrade. At 400 dollars a month of qualifying purchases, that's 4,800 dollars a year, a 96 dollar reward, and 31 dollars ahead of the 65 dollar upgrade, before tax. At 200 dollars a month, it's 2,400 dollars, a 48 dollar reward, and 17 dollars behind. Add sales tax to the fee and the break-even climbs. At Ontario's 13 percent HST, it's about 3,673 dollars a year. For context, Statistics Canada puts the average household's spending on food from stores in 2023 at 8,579 dollars. So the upgrade needs close to 40 percent of that amount, in food or any other qualifying merchandise, to go through Costco's registers just to pay for itself. If most of your Costco spending is on the excluded list, it counts for nothing toward the reward. One exception to the math. If you already place a Same-Day order of 150 dollars or more every month, the 10 dollar credit can add up to 120 dollars a year, enough to cover the upgrade by itself. But Same-Day prices are marked up, so count it only if you'd order that way anyway. The CIBC card's cash back comes from CIBC, separate from Costco's 2 percent reward. And Costco isn't the only membership on offer. On their Canadian pages, PC Optimum Insiders is 119 dollars a year plus tax, Walmart Plus is 89 dollars plus taxes, and Amazon Prime and Instacart Plus are 99 dollars a year each. None of them is a warehouse club, so compare what you'd actually use. If the numbers say no, the refund in number nine is in writing.

How to protect yourself. First, check your actual spending. Look at your Costco receipts for the last year. Take out gas, purchases at the pharmacies and food courts, tobacco, taxes and fees, because Costco's terms say the 2 percent doesn't count them. Second, do the Executive math. Multiply your qualifying spending by about 2 percent and subtract 65 dollars plus your province's tax. Below about 3,250 dollars a year, and more once tax is added, the reward alone won't cover the upgrade, unless you'd use the monthly Same-Day credit anyway. Remember the three-month cut-off and the 1,250 dollar cap. Third, compare unit prices with your usual grocer. Work out the price per 100 grams, per litre or per load for the things you actually buy at your usual grocer, and write down the store and the date. For Costco's side, use your receipts, since costco.ca prices can differ from the warehouse, and Costco's terms say recording of prices in any manner is not permitted. Fourth, know the refund rules before you pay. Costco will cancel and refund your fee if you're dissatisfied, the merchandise guarantee has written exceptions, and an Executive who downgrades, or cancels with a refund, gives up the reward.

Costco's terms are long, and they're printed. The renewal is the one purchase there where the fee is fixed, and the value isn't. Check your last reward coupon or your account, and tell us in the comments: Gold Star or Executive, roughly what you spent at the registers last year, and which province you're in. We'll do the arithmetic for a few of you. And remember, the truth is not always on the menu.

APPENDIX B — DO NOT SAY (on air, lower-thirds, description, pinned comment or thumbnail)

- Price-tag codes (asterisks, .97, .88, .00, death star, date codes). Any Kirkland supplier claim. Anything sourced to employees, insiders, Reddit, forums or deal blogs.
- Pharmacy, optical, hearing-aid or prescription items of any kind, beyond reading the word "pharmacies" or "optical centres" inside Costco's own exclusion text. Any food-safety or recall item.
- Gas-price gap claims ("5 to 15 cents cheaper", GasBuddy). Fuel-quality or engine claims.
- "The Executive 2% counts at the gas bar." "3% restaurants capped at $8,000" on the personal card. "Costco refunds the difference if the 2% doesn't cover the upgrade" (U.S. lore).
- Executive early hours in Canada (Costco's 10-K says U.S.).
- Coaching a refund play ("shop 11 months, cancel in month 12"). Promo-code membership resets.
- The renewal rate as a Canadian figure. Any combined U.S.+Canada or worldwide figure without its label.
- Any statement that the proposed class action has merit; it is an allegation, not certified.
- Any warehouse shelf price. All Costco prices in this video are costco.ca or Same-Day prices.
- "Secret", "hidden", "quietly", "caught", "exposed", "trouble", "scam", "trick", "hack".
- The Sept 17 "Costco Canada Just Got Caught" video as a source (it misdates a quote and misreads the exclusions).
- Cut after verification and not to be re-added: the Competition Bureau "five grocery giants" quote (it sat next to online pricing and implied a link the Bureau disclaims); the Canada monthly sales slowdown and the Abacus "seen as American" survey (no source links either to renewals); the fee increase's share of membership income growth (implied motive); "very few subscriptions keep nine in ten" (no benchmark); "gas is the biggest line on the statement" (no data); "you keep the item" on price adjustments (not on Costco's page); any claim that using the fee refund is abuse.
