Number two. The eco-fee case.

This one is in a court file, so I'm going to use the court's words.

In May twenty twenty-three, a class action was filed in the Superior Court of Quebec against Dollarama. The allegation, and it's an allegation, was that on products carrying an environmental handling fee, the fee was shown separately on the shelf label, in a much smaller font, and added at the checkout, so the price on the shelf was not the price you paid.

In April twenty twenty-four, Dollarama and the plaintiffs brought the judge a settlement: two and a half million dollars, paid in gift cards worth up to fifteen dollars. The judge refused it. Among the concerns on the record: one point two eight million people had pre-registered, so the per-person amount could have fallen below fifteen dollars, and the court raised the question of what would happen to the customer data collected through Dollarama's app in the process.

So they went back. On the third of December twenty twenty-four, the court approved a second settlement: two million six hundred and forty-three thousand seven hundred and eighteen dollars and seventy-five cents.
