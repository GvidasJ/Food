# VERIFICATION LOG — 8 Apple Varieties Sold in Canada To NEVER EAT Again! (Only 3 Are ACTUALLY Worth Buying)

Script: script_apples.md. Research: research_apples.md (eight dossiers, 29 Sep 2026). Log date: 29 Sep 2026.

## 1 Method

One adversarial pass by nine agents: six section verifiers (each claim checked against the dossiers and, where the dossier said so, the live URL) and three cross-cutting lenses (house rules and libel, arithmetic and structure, hostile knowledgeable viewer). Result: 693 claims checked, 254 flags (hard-error 61, unsupported 38, label 137, style 18). Many flags are the same defect found by two or three agents. The body was rewritten in one pass to apply every hard error and the label fixes listed in section 3, then trimmed to length. No second automated pass was run.

## 2 Roster and basis

Avoid list ranked by acres in Ontario (OAG 2025 Annual Report, 2024 tree census) plus B.C. (Ministry of Agriculture 2025 Tree Fruit Acreage and Production Report, 2026 insured acres), most planted first: Gala 4,793; Honeycrisp 3,137; Red Delicious 946; Fuji 438; Pink Lady 229 (B.C. only); Granny Smith 112 (B.C. only); Envy 0 on any list; Cosmic Crisp 0 (Washington-only licence to 26 May 2032). Worth-it, least to most: Spartan 373; McIntosh 2,268; Ambrosia 4,014. Quebec and Nova Scotia publish no variety acreage; the cold open now says so on air. Five February verdicts change (Ambrosia, Spartan, Cortland, Empire, Gala) and the cold open names all five.
