# Verification Log — Canadian Ground Beef script
Verified 2026-08-09. Safe phrasings binding on script_ground_beef.md.

## Regulatory backbone
| Claim | Verdict | Safe phrasing | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground beef = beef only | VERIFIED | Anything labelled "ground beef" in Canada must be beef and nothing else — no water, fillers, binders, preservatives | CFIA Canadian Standards of Identity Vol. 7 (Table 2 Part A); FDR B.14.015C |
| Fat tiers | VERIFIED | Extra lean ≤10%, lean ≤17%, medium ≤23%, regular ≤30% — maximums, not exact contents | CFIA meat labelling requirements |
| Burger naming loophole | VERIFIED | "Beef burger" may legally contain fillers (edible milk/egg/yeast/vegetable material) + water/salt/seasoning; "ground beef pattie" must be beef only; seasoned no-filler = "beef pattie" | CFIA labelling + Vol. 7 Items 8–9. Say "fillers such as wheat-based crumbs," not that the standard names wheat crumbs |
| Mechanically separated meat | PARTIAL | MSM cannot go into ground beef; BSE rules ban MSM from vertebral column of cattle over 30 months. NOT "banned in Canada" (that's the US rule) | CFIA SRM guidance; Vol. 7 |
| XL Foods 2012 | VERIFIED | Largest beef recall in Canadian history: E. coli at Brooks, Alberta; 1,800+ products, 18 confirmed illnesses, licence suspended, $4M class settlement, plant later to JBS (leased Oct 2012, bought 2013) | CBC; Globe and Mail |
| Origin labelling | PARTIAL | Imported meat in its retail package must say "Product of [country]"; beef processed/packaged in Canada need not carry foreign-origin label. Don't assert the legal "substantial transformation" test on air | CFIA country-of-origin; Globe and Mail |
| Imports | VERIFIED | 2024: ~429M lb beef imported (US 44.6%, Australia 14.4%, NZ 12.6%, Uruguay 5.6%); early 2025 imports +22% | Farm Credit Canada |
| Grass-fed | VERIFIED | No prescribed federal standard; policed only under general truth-in-labelling; certification is private | Law-firm analysis of CFIA guidance (pull CFIA method-of-production page before air) |
| Lean % accuracy testing | UNVERIFIED | OMITTED from script | — |
| CBC underweight meat | VERIFIED | CBC 2023–2025: Loblaw and Sobeys stores sold underweight meat (packaging weighed as meat), overcharges 2–16.7%. Weight ≠ fat-content accuracy — do not conflate | CBC |
| 71°C | VERIFIED | Ground beef safe at internal 71°C; colour unreliable. (Poultry differs — don't generalize) | Health Canada |

## Claims killed
- Any "ground beef contains fillers" claim for Canada — illegal, so the script's whole angle is the burger-label loophole instead.
- Costco Canada 2025 recall — does not exist.
- Compliments recall as recent — 2013 only.
- "Mechanically separated beef banned in Canada" — US rule, corrected.
- Loblaw/Metro/Sobeys "100% Canadian beef" — never claimed by them; only Walmart has the verified claim.
- Webers entry — insufficient sourcing.
- US LFTB/"pink slime" reclassification — US-only; not imported into Canadian claims.
