# Verification Log — "Grass-Fed Butter / 7 Labels" script
Verified 2026-08-12 by two research agents (76 + 87 tool calls). Safe phrasings binding on script_butter.md. Source video: Consumer Exposed grass-fed butter countdown (5iTaBUa3Kq8), full transcript read; structure adapted (7 avoid + shelf check + 3 winners), all facts re-verified for Canada.

## Regulatory backbone
| Claim | Verdict | Safe phrasing / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Butter standard | VERIFIED | ≥80% milk fat; permitted: milk solids, bacterial culture, salt. Cite "Canada's federal food standards" generically — FDR B.08.056 REPEALED Dec 2024 (SOR/2024-244); standard now CFIA Canadian Food Compositional Standards Vol. 7 s.7.3.1. (B.08.057 was whey butter — never cite it for butter) |
| "Food colour" in butter | CAUTION | Old B.08.056 explicitly permitted food colour; current s.7.3.1 enumerated list omits it, but additive-table status untraced. Script only says annatto is "legal… declared… harmless" — do NOT claim it's now impermissible |
| EU butterfat | VERIFIED (nuance) | EU legal minimum is ALSO 80% (Reg 1308/2013 Annex VII App II). 82% = EU intervention spec / typical French norm. Script says "typically churned to 82 or a touch higher… EU legal minimum is the same 80" — keep this phrasing, never "EU requires 82" |
| Grass-fed regulation | VERIFIED | CFIA method-of-production claims page: claims "must be accurate, truthful, and must not mislead or deceive the consumer"; substantiation (third-party audit, valid documentation, or non-gov cert) held by company, shown to inspector on request; NO pre-market review; NO federal definition of grass-fed for dairy |
| DFC voluntary grass-fed standard | VERIFIED | "At least 75% of the total dry matter intake (DMI) of the lactating herd must consist of forage throughout the year"; remaining 25% grains/supplements incl. corn silage; third-party certified. DFO (Ontario) mirror standard exists |
| Blue cow logo | VERIFIED | Certifies 100% Canadian milk + proAction; says nothing about feed |
| Butter import tariff | VERIFIED | Over-access butter tariff 298.5% (min $4/kg) — Snopes/FactCheck.org/CBSA D10-18-7. Say "priced out," NOT "banned"; within-quota imports duty-free but tiny/allocated |
| StatCan avg price | VERIFIED | Table 18-10-0245-01: 454g butter ~$5.52–5.99 through 2025–mid-2026 (June 2026 = $5.94) → "running under six dollars" |
| CDC support price | VERIFIED | Butter support price $10.3489 → $10.5662/kg (~2.1%) effective Feb 1, 2026 → script's "about two percent" |
