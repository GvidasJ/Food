## VERDICT KEY
- **VERIFIED** — reliable secondary source (major outlet; company's own materials for claims about itself)
- **VERIFIED-PRIMARY** — primary source (Justice Laws, CFIA/Health Canada, Finance Canada, StatCan pulled directly, recall database, company release)
- **ATTRIBUTED** — company/organization claim presented as their claim
- **DERIVED** — our arithmetic on verified inputs (inputs shown)
- **UNVERIFIED** — must NOT appear in voiceover
- **EXCLUDED** — deliberately left out (reason given)
