## MANDATORY PHRASINGS (binding on the voiceover — do not paraphrase these away)

2. **Clover Leaf's innocence:** say affirmatively, on camera: "Clover Leaf — the Canadian company — was never charged with anything, by anyone." Its links are common ownership and the parallel CCAA filing only.
3. **Chicken of the Sea:** "says it blew the whistle and got leniency for cooperating; it was never charged." Never "guilty", never "admitted a crime".
4. **Canadian courts:** "Ontario courts threw it out, saying the plaintiffs hadn't shown a basis in fact that it extended to Canada" + the both-ways sentence: "nothing proven about Canada, in either direction."
5. **Competition Bureau:** exactly "we found no record of any Competition Bureau case" — never "the Bureau cleared them", never "there was no conspiracy here".
6. **Civil settlements (if used at all):** "settled for money without admitting the allegations" — settlements are not admissions. (Currently EXCLUDED from script — see below.)
7. **Tariff scope:** "that's the empty can, not the fish" + "US-origin cans" + "cans made in Asia aren't touched." Never say tuna is tariffed.
8. **July price jump:** "right as the tariff war heated up. Correlation, not proof."
9. **Mercury:** only the attributed construction: "Health Canada's mercury guidance says…" with its verbatim content; add "That's Health Canada's advice, not ours." No independent safety language anywhere else in the episode.
10. **Kirkland albacore:** both caveats together: "American reporting about American cans; we couldn't confirm who packs the Canadian ones."
11. **Selection vegetable broth:** the fairness triple: legal ("the federal compositional standard specifically allows vegetable broth"), declared ("printed right on the label"), common ("common practice in canneries around the world") — then the reading-test framing. No adulteration language.
12. **Dolphin friendly:** "no defined federal standard… policed only under the general ban on false or misleading labels… the company has to be able to back it up." Never say any specific brand's claim is false.
13. **St. Jean's ownership:** "majority ownership belongs to a partnership of five Nuu-chah-nulth First Nations" with "since 2015–16" — entity names vary across sources, keep it at partnership level. Founding year 1961, never 1960.
14. **Ordering disclaimer:** on air, early: "nobody publishes a sales chart for tuna in this country, so we've ordered the store brands by the size of the chains that sell them."
15. **"Only tuna canning operation in Canada":** attribute — "by its own brand's account" / per Raincoast's release. Never "last major tuna cannery in North America".
16. **Clover Leaf market share:** the script deliberately avoids the 46% figure; if ever added, it must carry "as of 2009, per its then-owner's securities filings".
