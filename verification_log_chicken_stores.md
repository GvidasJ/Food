## Regulatory backbone
| Claim | Verdict | Safe phrasing / notes |
|---|---|---|
| "Seasoned" naming rule | VERIFIED-PRIMARY | CFIA Industry Labelling Tool: "seasoned" acceptable when phosphate salts alone or with water are added; water alone → "water added" in common name. Type 1/2 products with phosphates/water MUST declare "% meat protein" in the common name (min half letter size, 1.6mm) |
| % meat protein as dilution meter | VERIFIED | Plain breast ≈ 21-23% protein; declared minimum reveals solution load. US contrast: USDA 2014 rule requires "% added solution"; Canada requires min meat protein % |
| Retained water declaration | VERIFIED-PRIMARY | Three exact CFIA phrasings quoted in script verbatim ("up to X% water retained" etc.); mandatory >0.5% |
| 8% retained water allowance | VERIFIED | CFIA Poultry Water Retention Control Program: carcasses <2.3 kg up to 8% (≥2.3 kg: 6%; parts: 8%) without % declaration. Script: "up to eight percent on smaller birds" |
| Air-chilled claim control | VERIFIED | Plants need validated written PCP examined by CFIA to label "air chilled"/"no water added" |
| Hormone ban | VERIFIED-PRIMARY | Illegal since March 4, 1963 (Canadian Food Focus; CFC FAQ verbatim "illegal in Canada since the 1960s"). CFIA: bare "hormone free" NOT acceptable; approved wording = "like other chickens…" |
| Zabiha Halal honest qualifier | VERIFIED-PRIMARY | zabihahalal.com verbatim: "Raised without the use of added hormones or steroids. Like all chicken in Canada." + air chilled, 100% chicken |
| "Grain-fed" = table stakes | VERIFIED | CFIA: grain-fed claim needs ≥85% grain diet; CFC: grain is the main feed for ALL Canadian chicken |
| RWA meaning + antibiotic timeline | VERIFIED | CFIA: no antibiotics birth→slaughter by any method. Industry: Category I preventive use ended May 15 2014; Category II end of 2018 (CFC FAQ) |
| Raised by a Canadian Farmer logo | VERIFIED | Mandatory, third-party-audited national OFFS + Animal Care programs, ~2,800 farms; certifies Canadian origin/baseline, NOT RWA/organic/air-chilled |
| Tariff wall | VERIFIED | Over-quota ~238% whole birds up to ~249% cuts — script says "238 to 249 percent depending on the cut" (mandatory range phrasing) |
| Chlorine washes | EXCLUDED | NOT banned in Canada (Health Canada LONOs permit ~20-50 ppm hypochlorite; 2017 CFIA survey 59% of plants used chlorine-based products). Script deliberately makes NO chlorine claim |
