## 4. INTERPRETATIONS (compiler's readings, flagged by construction)

- "The chain Canadians complain about most turned out to be the cheapest per millilitre" — Tim Hortons is the only chain with a verified price and a company volume; "cheapest" is within the verifiable set, and the script says McCafé is not ranked until measured.
- "Six of the ten publish no price anywhere online" — Country Style, Robin's, Coffee Time, Presse Café, Van Houtte Café, A&W (own sites/ordering systems returned no price on 23 Sept). Starbucks is counted separately ("would not show me a price… unless I signed in"). McCafé's prices come from releases.
- "Worst delivery mark-up on the list" — Tim Hortons +25.1% at Toronto/Cobourg vs Second Cup +16.9%; only two chains had a measurable in-app delivery uplift.
- "Six of these ten aren't who you think" (ownership) — Tim Hortons (RBI/3G, Miami), McCafé (Chicago), Starbucks (Seattle), Van Houtte Café (KDP brand / MTY cafés), Country Style (MTY), Second Cup (Foodtastic). Coffee Time and Robin's are Chairman's Brands, Toronto; A&W and Presse Café are Canadian-owned.
- Ranking of the seven is by the gap between price disclosure and coffee disclosure, weakest record first; the script states the rule in the yardstick.
