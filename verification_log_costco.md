## Material changes

1. **Arithmetic.** "About a third of an average household's grocery spending" was wrong: $3,250 ÷ $8,579 = 37.9%. Now "close to 40 percent of that amount, in food or any other qualifying merchandise". The tax example now names Ontario's 13% HST. Every break-even line now says "before tax" or "more once tax is added", and the reward is "approximately 2 percent", Costco's word.
2. **Price basket.** "In Mississauga" was true for only four of the seven channels; now stated precisely. "Treat them as a floor" (an implied warehouse-price claim) cut; replaced with Costco's own words: costco.ca includes shipping, Same-Day is marked up, Costco publishes no shelf prices. Losses corrected: no conventional large eggs were available on Same-Day (dropped as a "loss"); tuna and rice flagged as not like-for-like; chicken stated as more than Walmart and a No Frills special. "Tin" and "100 percent Colombian" removed (not in the captures). Promotions on both sides disclosed.
3. **Implied-motive juxtapositions cut.** The Competition Bureau "five grocery giants" quote (it sat between online markups and the class action), the Canadian monthly sales slowdown, the Abacus "seen as American" survey, and the fee increase's share of income growth were all cut from the renewal discussion. "Very few subscriptions keep nine in ten" (no benchmark) replaced with Costco's own history (89.7% in FY2012 to 92.3% now, U.S. and Canada together). "Almost everyone renews" now carries the U.S.+Canada label.
4. **Class action.** Now also says "no court has ruled on it" and that Costco has moved to strike it.
5. **Executive extras.** The $10 Instacart credit now carries its "eligible products" and "can be changed or cancelled at any time" terms, and the turn adds the honest exception: up to $120 a year if you'd place a $150+ Same-Day order every month anyway. Same-Day orders are flagged as not on the 2% earning list.
6. **Early hours.** "If you've heard otherwise, that's the American program" (only 3 of 115 warehouse pages checked) replaced with the 10-K's U.S. wording plus "the three Canadian warehouse pages we checked... check your own warehouse's page".
7. **Reward rules.** "Nothing is lost" corrected (purchases roll to next year only if still Executive); "decide before the cut-off" (unsupported timing advice) replaced with "count the reward you'd give up first"; the coupon's redemption exclusions now include alcohol, tobacco, travel and services; Costco Travel's "directly from Costco Travel" and "qualified purchases" conditions restored; Quebec's added pharmacy and optical exclusions read from Costco's list.
8. **Card.** The 2014 Amex end date attributed to The Canadian Press; the May restaurant-cap correction now says the $8,000 restaurant cap is in the Business card's terms; the $65 new-member offer now carries its same-day and $65-in-120-days conditions; payment methods attributed to the customer-service page with "preprinted cheques, conditions apply"; "points" → "cash back".
9. **Price adjustment.** "You keep the item" (not on Costco's page) replaced with "ask at the membership counter"; the demo-model exclusion restored; online orders noted as having their own policy; the costco.ca small-order grocery surcharge added.
10. **Bundled items.** Warranty attributed to the Technical and Warranty Service page with its "when the manufacturer's warranty is shorter" condition; tire warranty "not available for all vehicles and tires"; tire promotions dated "when we checked on October 1st" with "four or more" and "conditions apply"; Shop Card sentence cut (it misstated a purchase range as a reload range); Thanksgiving closure limited to the pages checked.
11. **Refund and terms.** "Not a way to get a free year" (implied that using the guarantee is abuse) replaced with "we won't coach a refund play"; May's "reward deducted from the refund" corrected on air; the amendment clause restored to "without prior written notice or consent", with the B.C. rule stated correctly and the "version that binds you" legal conclusion cut.
12. **Other.** "Collected" → "took in... membership-fee revenue across all its countries" (label in the same sentence); operating income said in U.S. dollars; scan clause no longer says "now"; app card accepted; 16+ wording restored; May corrections widened to the gas-price gap and member count; the order basis now names item 10; "pharmacy" removed from the viewer's own checklist except where reading Costco's exclusion; disclaimer under 40 words and names the Federal Court docket.
