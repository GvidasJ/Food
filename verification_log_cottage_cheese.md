# Verification log — cottage cheese redo (script_cottage_cheese.md)

Run: 30 Sep 2026. Nine adversarial verifiers (six section checkers, a libel/house-rules lens, an arithmetic/structure lens, a hostile-viewer lens) read the draft (commit c744913) against the six research dossiers in research_cottage_cheese.md and, where load-bearing, the live sources.

Totals: 521 claims checked; 143 flags (32 hard-error, 20 unsupported, 69 label, 22 style). Every flag was dispositioned. None was left open in the body; items that need a pre-recording check are in Appendix C of the script.

Final body: 21,716 characters (paragraph sum), mid-list ask at 61.4% of body characters, tagline twice, disclosure once, four-step close, no asterisks, no sentence over 40 words.

## Material changes

1. **Title.** The draft title 'Canadians Must AVOID These 9 "Cottage Cheese" Brands (3 That Are ACTUALLY Real Cottage Cheese)' was replaced with 'Canadians Must AVOID These 9 Cottage Cheese Brands (And 3 With 3 Ingredients Or Less)'. Reason (house-rules-libel-0/1, hostile-viewer-5): eight of the nine tubs lawfully print the prescribed name "cottage cheese"; scare quotes plus "ACTUALLY Real" implied unlawful use of a standardized name, which no regulator has found. "And 3", not "Only 3", because Farm Boy's pressed tub also passes the test.
2. **The "real" framing is gone from the body.** "Three tubs that read exactly like the law" and "the cottage cheese that reads like the law" were false for the dry-curd picks (microbial enzyme is not in 7.4.5) and implied breach by the other nine. Now: "some of the shortest ingredient lists we found"; the enzyme allowance is named as the channel's own; the turn concedes the picks are dry or pressed curd and not like for like.
3. **Every legal-conclusion sentence removed.** "So the front is fine", "normal and legal for a store brand", "Legal cottage cheese at a low price", "Both lists are legal", "Every item on these tubs is declared" — none had a source; compliance is a CFIA determination. Replaced with "We are not saying any of these lists breaks a rule" and label-only statements.
4. **Unverified additive-list claims removed.** "The rest arrive through the separate additive lists" and "Everything else comes in through a separate list" asserted Health Canada List 4/5 rows that were only tool-summarised (xanthan gum, polysorbate 80, carbon dioxide, lactase unverified). Now: "The other eight are not named in that paragraph"; "Additives come in through Health Canada's separate lists."
5. **Canadian Dairy Commission half-percent line cut.** The CDC page is dated 2022-10-05 and describes the repealed rule. The script now cites the old regulation's 0.5 per cent cap as history only.
6. **No Name count corrected** from three tubs to four (store 1080 listed 500 g, 2% 750 g, 1% 750 g, 1% Light 500 g; three distinct lines). The 1% 750 g tub is now accounted for.
7. **Compliments 5.59 list.** The draft read the page-text list as that tub's list; the pack image on the same page differs. The script now says only that the page text and pack photo disagree, and that neither matches the 5.15 list. The tapioca/colour sentence was cut (it also dropped xanthan gum and singled out two additives).
8. **Baxter.** "46 percent more ... under another Saputo name" and "a price that moves with the logo" were contradicted by the research's own captures: Giant Tiger's website listed Baxter 2% at $4.98 ($1.00/100 g), the same as Neilson on Voilà; Atlantic Superstore Halifax had $7.49. 9. **Sealtest.** "Borrowed" → "licensed, not Agropur's own". "Best-known" (no share data) → "first name". "Small print" framing cut; the Loblaw front image shows "COTTAGE CHEESE PRODUCT" on the front. Northumberland match now specifies the 1% line on Sealtest's own site and the 2% front on Voilà. Amqui: "its main plant" → "the main plant in the country" (Radio-Canada's wording); "the lock-out ended" → "workers accepted a deal on November 21st". Mailhot's full title restored; sales figure tied to cottage cheese per the article's dek.
10. **Natrel.** The CFIA best-practice sentence and the "$8.9 billion co-op" closer were cut: together they implied the label falls short of guidance, with no CFIA view on record.
11. **Nordica.** "New Toronto plant" → expansion of the Clayson Road plant (release wording). "Claims record sales" → "says it had". 12. **Beatrice.** Tariff sentence cut (nothing ties Beatrice to imports; it insinuated). "Same items, same order" / "same list" corrected to "every item but one has the same name". Common-maker hint replaced by "A matching list does not tell you who makes a tub." Lactalis 1997 history no longer implies Beatrice arrived that year. Closer now says ownership is not the test.
