## MANDATORY PHRASINGS (binding on voiceover — do not paraphrase)
1. The paradox beat: present Nestlé's own page ("their words") + CFIA guidance verbatim, then "read your own canister… and see exactly which words Nestlé prints on the retail label." 2. Sodium caseinate/modified milk ingredients: always explained as "milk protein… stripped out of milk" — a composition fact. No health framing of caseinate, palm oil, coconut oil, or any additive, ever. Palm oil is named only as what the label says.

6. Prices: every figure "at time of filming"; the title's "ACTUALLY Cheaper" claim must be re-validated with filmed shelf tags before publish (claim #32) — if the day's prices flip, soften to "costs the same."
