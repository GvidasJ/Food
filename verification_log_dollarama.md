## 1. THE STRUCTURAL DECISION

Six Canadian channels have made this video. Every one of them built it on the same three unsourced beats: a copied US cereal example in ounces, "8.2 million customers a week" (in no filing), and the 2022 Environmental Defence chemical testing (health, out under house rules). Not one priced a single item with a date, store and size.

This script is built on three things none of them had: Dollarama's own price list (1,011 items captured from shop.dollarama.com on 21 Sept 2026), Dollarama's own filings (FY2026 annual report, AIF, proxy circular, Q2 FY2027 release of 16 Sept 2026), and the Quebec Superior Court record of the eco-fee class action. The thesis — Dollarama wins 17 of 28 like-for-like but only 5 of 28 against the cheapest way to buy — is the honest result, both halves spoken.
