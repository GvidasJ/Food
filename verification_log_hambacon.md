# VERIFICATION LOG — HAM & BACON EPISODE (post date: September 10, 2026)

Companion to script_hambacon.md and research_hambacon.md (Parts 1–3). Every scripted claim below carries a verdict. The MANDATORY PHRASINGS section is binding on the voiceover — do not paraphrase those lines in ways that change their meaning.

VERDICT KEY
- VERIFIED-PRIMARY — confirmed against a primary source (Justice Laws, CFIA, Health Canada, StatCan, SEC/SEDAR, company's own filings/press releases/product pages)
- VERIFIED — confirmed against reliable secondary sources (CBC, Globe and Mail, AP, DCB, The Canadian Encyclopedia)
- ATTRIBUTED — presented on air as someone's statement, not as our fact ("the company says…", "the poll found…")
- DERIVED — arithmetic we performed on verified numbers; show the math on screen
- UNVERIFIED — do not state as fact; use only with the exact hedge specified
- EXCLUDED — researched and deliberately kept out of the script

## MANDATORY PHRASINGS (binding on voiceover)

1. 2008 death toll — say EXACTLY: "The federal government's independent investigator put the toll at 22 deaths. Maple Leaf's own history page today says 23 tragic deaths." Never say a bare "23" or "22" without attribution. Never use 20 or 24 on air.
2. 2008 legal framing — must include: "No charges were ever involved. This was a recall, an apology, and a civil settlement." Never imply criminal wrongdoing; there was none.
3. McCain quotes — caption only from the archival footage you screen, not from print reconstructions (one clause of the TV apology is garbled in the print record). The lawyers/accountants line: paraphrase as scripted, do not present as verbatim.
4. Store-brand packers — never state or imply who currently packs PC, No Name, Great Value, or Compliments. "One plant. Dozens of labels." is 2008 historical record only. The July 2026 FSIS notice shows only that Maple Leaf packs other names for the US market — an import-paperwork recall, no safety framing.
5. Celery extract — label-reading only, both directions: "We're not telling you either style is better or worse." The nitrite equivalence is attributed to CBC Marketplace's lab test and CFIA's cultured-ingredients guidance. No health commentary on nitrites anywhere in the episode.
6. Sodium — only printed label values with mg + %DV + printed serving size (Maple Leaf 310 mg/13%/2 slices 50 g is the only one scripted); everything else re-read from physical packs on camera. CNF figures are government per-100g data used for the FOP arithmetic — label them "federal nutrient data" on screen.
7. FOP basis — say "per serving or per the official reference amount, whichever is greater." NEVER say "assessed per 50 grams." Before naming any specific product as wearing/escaping the symbol, verify that physical package on camera.
8. Smithfield era — ownership facts only. No suggestion of wrongdoing by Smithfield, the Schneider family, or Maple Leaf in the takeover fight. "Hostile takeover bid" is a neutral technical term — keep it attached to the bid, not to character.
9. Schneiders buyback figure — only "roughly half a billion dollars including debt," anchored to Smithfield's SEC filing. Do not use $378M, $413M, or $515M variants on air.
10. Vallée-Jonction — company-attributed throughout ("with the company's president saying…"). No blame framing toward Olymel, unions, or governments.
11. Hogtown/Davies — keep the hedges: "was said to be the largest," "Toronto's food lore credits." Never "was the largest" or "invented" as flat fact.
12. Bacon-over-sex poll — must keep "It was the company's own poll, so season to taste" (or equivalent attribution) in the same breath.
13. Tariffs — pork into the US stayed effectively tariff-free via CUSMA (do not claim US tariffs hit bacon); China's 25% is the live one. Never mention "Section 338."
14. Great Value origin qualifier — the on-air use is conditional on re-reading the physical pack; if the pack differs, drop the beat.
15. Schneiders Country Naturals — if mentioned at all (currently not scripted): "we couldn't find it on shelves," never "discontinued."
16. Black Forest / peameal absences — phrase as "appears in no federal standard" / "has no legal definition," never "illegal" or "unregulated product" (general food law still applies).
17. Prices — every price on air carries "when we checked" or "at time of research" and the retailer name; re-verify all on filming day.
