# Verification Log — "17 Ice Cream Brands" script
Verified 2026-08-10. Safe phrasings binding on script_ice_cream.md. (Gap-check agent results appended at bottom.)

## Regulatory backbone
| Claim | Verdict | Safe phrasing / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ice cream standard | VERIFIED | Canadian ice cream must be a frozen dairy food with ≥10% milk fat (8% if chocolate/fruit/nuts added). Cite "federal Food and Drug Regulations" generically — old section B.08.062 was REPEALED Dec 18 2024; standard now lives in Canadian Food Compositional Standards Vol. 7 / CFIA Standards of Identity Vol. 1 |
| "Frozen dessert" naming | VERIFIED | Products using vegetable oils (palm/coconut) instead of enough cream can't be called ice cream — sold beside it as "frozen dessert" (non-standardized name). "Frozen dairy dessert" is a separate defined standard |
| "Ice milk" | VERIFIED (listings) | A distinct lower-fat designation used on budget tubs (No Name, Selection, Great Value 4L) |
| Modified milk ingredients | VERIFIED | Catch-all for altered milk components (whey, casein, protein concentrates), often cheaper than fresh cream/milk; some importable outside fluid-milk tariff limits (McGill OSS; CBC) |
| Tub shrinkflation | PARTIAL | 2L → 1.66L → 1.5L documented (CBC; Globe "ice cream tax" — sub-500mL tubs become taxable). Don't pin a specific year to the shrink |
| Labelling confusion cite | VERIFIED | CBC News 2017 quiz "Ice cream or frozen dessert?" — NOT a Marketplace episode; don't attribute to Marketplace |

## Ownership corrections (binding)
- Breyers & Ben & Jerry's: owned by The Magnum Ice Cream Company — Unilever spinoff, Amsterdam-listed Dec 8 2025. NOT "Unilever" anymore.
- Nestlé Canada still operates Canadian ice cream (Häagen-Dazs licence, Real Dairy, Parlour via Froneri licence arrangement per listing); Feb 19 2026: sale of remaining ice cream business to Froneri announced, phased to early 2027 — say "announced, being transferred," never "sold."
- The 2019-2020 US$4B Froneri deal was US-only.
