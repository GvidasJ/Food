## MANDATORY PHRASINGS (binding on VO — the tier discipline IS the video)

1. **Never say Loblaw was fined, charged, or convicted for bread.** The verified frame: admitted participation → cooperation → "full immunity from prosecution." The fine belongs to Canada Bread alone.
2. **"Fifteen increases" and "$1.50 a loaf"** are always "allegations from investigation filings, as reported" — never adjudicated facts. The only convicted conduct: Canada Bread's two increases (2007, 2011). The only admitted retail conduct: Loblaw/Weston participation 2001-2015.
3. **Sobeys, Metro, Walmart, Giant Tiger, Maple Leaf, Canada-Bread-era disputes:** every mention carries denial + "no charges have ever been laid" + presumption of innocence. The recap's fairness paragraph is not cuttable.
4. **Property controls:** the Bureau's line "there is no conclusion of wrongdoing at this time" is spoken verbatim; Loblaw's June 2025 move is a "voluntary commitment" the Bureau is "monitoring" — never a finding, never a punishment.
5. **The meat:** Loblaw's own words only — "error," "we apologize for these errors." NEVER "isolated incident" (they didn't say it). Manitoba suit always carries "not certified, allegations unproven."
6. **No Name freeze:** "experts quoted by CBC called it a PR strategy" — attributed analysis, and Metro's standard-practice quote stays as the balance.
7. **Committee math:** "$1 per $25 basket" is Weston's; "$1 million a day in excess profits" is Singh's characterization, rejected by Weston. Never swap or blend them.
8. **"Misguided" is Galen Weston's word** (AGM May 2, 2024) — never attributed to Per Bank.
9. **Boycott impact:** only Loblaw's own words ("minor," "a bit of an impact in certain stores") + the 0.2% vs 6.1% contrast; never "the boycott cost Loblaw X." Code-signing timing: both facts stated ("two weeks into the boycott" + "the company says those two events are unrelated").
10. **Greedflation:** the word itself is not used in VO; Dalhousie vs Stanford both attributed; Bureau's "modest yet meaningful" carries the official middle. "Nobody has ruled greed illegal" stays.
11. **PC Optimum:** quote the OPC findings in the regulator's language with the disposition labels ("well-founded and resolved" / "well-founded and conditionally resolved") + Loblaw's disagreement and 12-month assessment commitment.

13. **Shrinkflation:** StatCan's 29.6% is the only hard claim; PC examples are "journalism and consumer documentation, not a regulator's ruling"; "it's legal" stays.
