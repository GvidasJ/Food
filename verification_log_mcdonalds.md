# VERIFICATION LOG — McDONALD'S CANADA EPISODE (format test 2: documented commentary)

Companion to script_mcdonalds.md and research_mcdonalds.md (Parts 1–3). This episode's entire premise is that every "wrong" is documented — the log is therefore the product. The MANDATORY PHRASINGS section is binding on the voiceover.

VERDICT KEY
- VERIFIED-PRIMARY — company releases/SEC filings/StatCan/official datasets/museum-government pages, quoted or pulled directly
- VERIFIED — reliable outlets (CBC, CTV, CNBC, CNN, Canadian Press, Globe, trade press)
- ATTRIBUTED — presented as someone's statement/claim/survey/memory, with the source named in the VO
- DERIVED — arithmetic on verified numbers; show the math on screen
- UNVERIFIED — only with the exact hedge specified
- EXCLUDED — researched, deliberately kept out

## MANDATORY PHRASINGS (binding on voiceover)

1. EVERY "wrong" HAS A NAME ATTACHED. Surveys carry the firm's name; complaints carry the outlet that reported them; corporate admissions carry the call or release. If a sentence in the VO asserts a problem and names no source, cut the sentence.
2. THE CANADA SENTIMENT QUOTE never airs without the CEO's caveat that it had not transferred to the McDonald's brand. Non-negotiable; the script pairs them.
3. NO health commentary anywhere. The E. coli chapter and the GLP-1 material from the reference video are EXCLUDED in full. Menu items are missed, priced, discontinued — never judged nutritionally.
4. NO shrinkflation section. No Canadian sourcing met the standard. The word does not appear in the script; keep it that way.
5. NO implication of illegality, deception, or bad faith by anyone: price levels, loyalty changes, franchisee disputes, and the profit margin are lawful business facts. The required sentences stay in: "Nothing about that is illegal or even unusual" (profit) and "nobody's villain, everybody's margin" (franchisees).
6. US figures are labelled US every time: Erlinger's 40%, the $4.39→$5.29 Big Mac, the 2032 fountain target, the franchisee survey, the "simplify operations" quote. Never let a US number wear a Canadian flag.
7. Canada-specific sales/comps DO NOT EXIST publicly — never say "McDonald's Canada's sales rose/fell." The IOM segment may be referenced only as "the international segment that includes Canada" (not currently scripted).
8. Customer quotes (Narcity thread, SmartCanucks memories) are always framed as reported comments/memories via the named outlet — never as data, never as our findings.
9. The two fact-check beats (McPizza's last stores in Ohio/WV; the 2017 orange-drink story being American) are features — do not soften them; they are what separates this video from the sloppy versions.
10. The balance section survives every edit, whole: the price promise + Swijtink quotes, Best Burgers 2019, Buetikofer, poutine, McHappy Day/RMHC, the beef claim, the scale paragraph. Cutting balance for runtime converts documented commentary into a hit piece and breaks the channel's contract.
11. Kandahar is never mentioned (it was Tim Hortons). The Big-Mac-at-65-cents Richmond memorabilia menu is never captioned as 1967. No McHappy Day ordinal. No "633 locations" McPizza claim unless blogTO's text is captured first.
12. Vintage clips (Retrontario/RetroWinnipeg/Nostalgia Canadiana): licensing/fair-dealing review before any clip airs; the approved fallback for the cold open is a freeze-frame with VO description ("That was a real McDonald's commercial...") if the moving clip can't clear.
13. Edit-week re-pulls: StatCan CPI vectors and the coffee/beef rows (Aug 2026 CPI mid-September); any new McDonald's quarter (Q3 2026 reports late Oct — if it lands before post, add one line, attributed); the price-promise terms (still in effect?); Economist dataset for any new edition.
14. Prices are dated on screen, always: "Jan 2017," "July 2026 edition," "at time of research."
