# Verification log — script_nofrills.md (Oct 5, 2026)

Verification ran on Oct 3, 2026 against the committed draft. Seven parallel checkers were used:

- three lenses (house rules and libel; arithmetic and structure; hostile viewer).

## Material changes

1. **Hard errors fixed:**
   - The "75 new locations" are grocery stores *and pharmacies*. Only the grocery expansion is "focused predominantly" on No Frills and Maxi.
   - The pickup-data counts now use one base: 352 unique locations, of which 212 are in Ontario, none in Quebec, and 143 say pickup is unavailable.
   - The "no price matching for PC Express" count is **63**, not 61. The dossier's 61 did not reproduce on recount.
   - "Prepared in Canada" *is* a CFIA-recognised claim, for food entirely prepared in Canada. The draft wrongly said it was not.
   - The "read the package" fix wrongly implied the CFIA's rules don't cover shelf signs. They do, and both penalties were for shelf displays.
   - The two CFIA fines were at stores *under Loblaw banners*. Fortinos is franchised, so "Loblaw-owned" was wrong.
2. **Ownership consistency.** Every "Loblaw-owned No Frills" now reads "a No Frills", because the AIF lists 0 corporate and 336 franchised stores. The basket stores are "stores under Loblaw banners". Franchisee first names were removed from the price comparisons, and stores are identified by street.
3. **Fairness and libel:**
   - The Ford item now includes the premier's office response and Loblaw's response. The meeting is placed "at the premier's home".
   - The Q2 2024 boycott-quarter figure now carries the same report's context: food inflation 1.7% vs 9.1%, traffic up, and food sales growth led by Maxi and No Frills. The "we're just giving you both" wink is cut.
   - The meat line now separates CBC's late-2024 Calgary finding from Loblaw's 2023 admission, which doesn't name No Frills.
   - The account-notice lines keep Loblaw's "varied by customer" qualifier. The unsourced "copycat messages" line is replaced with Loblaw's own caution, and "health information" is removed.
   - The basket item now says it is one day, 15 items, website prices only, with Loblaw's "may vary by online channel".
   - The multi-buy item no longer says limits "replaced" multi-buys. It says limit badges were at most 3 per store in the sample, and gives the full count of 7 multi-buy badges across four No Frills.
4. **Title and thumbnail.** The template-exact title without "Some" is withdrawn, and the "ARE THEY LEAVING?" thumbnail text is cut.
5. **Corrections said on air.** "Our December video said Loblaw owns FreshCo. It doesn't. Sobeys does."
6. **Unit prices.** Every on-air price now has a unit price: Cheerios, eggs by store, ketchup, peanut butter, peas and diced tomatoes.
7. **Verbatim and attribution fixes:**
   - the No Frills guarantee wording;
   - the Global News ESL statement;
   - "commonly known as the Average System";
   - the bread period, "late 2001 to March 2015";
   - EQB as the buyer;
   - the August 26 statement attributed to Global News;
   - the Bureau examination's scope;
   - the 50% sticker reason, now in Loblaw's own words;
   - "yellow stickers" removed.
8. **Cut for length or accuracy:**
   - the StatCan 77.6% / 22.4% split (it reads as a contradiction of the 29.6% figure);
   - charity donation of points;
   - the Insiders "closed" line;
   - online-order refund timings;
   - the CFIA $15,000 ceiling (the 2026 budget outcome is unchecked);
   - the five-year scale examinations;
   - the Bureau margin finding;
   - the PCO survey line;
   - the $24.11 payout;
   - the Measurement Canada "resolve it first" advice.
