## 2. Attributed claims and allegations (how each is labelled in the script)

- Kumar v. Salov: allegation ("a shopper sued… arguing"), defence (company's own words), procedural ruling ("a judge let the case proceed"), settlement ("settled in 2017… no court found the label false. Nothing was admitted"). The word "admitted" is never used for Salov.
- Koller v. Deoleo: allegation ("the shopper alleged"), denial ("Deoleo denied every allegation"), settlement ($7M; "shall not be used as an admission of any fault" quoted). No wrongdoing stated.
- AGCM 2016: "fined… over three oils… that its testing graded as virgin"; "administrative fine, in Italy, about grade, not about adulteration"; company response quoted; appeal status flagged as unconfirmed.
- UC Davis 2011: results stated with funding, age, market and the university's own disclaimer; "no foreign oil was found"; never applied to Canadian bottles.
- CFIA: "the report does not name a single brand" stated twice; no brand is linked to any CFIA result.
- Corporate claims attributed: Bertolli ("the company says… recycled plastic"; FAQ blending), Filippo Berio (FAQ sourcing), Costco (sustainability page), Terra Delyssa (own page), retailer copy for PC ("crafted in Italy"), Walmart listing ("a product of Spain").

## 3. Interpretations (ours, stated as ours)

- "The staircase" (same price for refined and extra virgin at no name, Great Value, Bertolli, Filippo Berio) is a price observation from P2 §4; the script says "the price gap… is zero", not that consumers are deceived.
- "Reaching for the wrong rung feels like the same purchase" is framed as our reading of shelf design, with "not because anybody broke a law in this country".
- "If the bottle is still priced like 2024, that is a decision the store made" is an inference from StatCan averages vs shelf prices; stated as such.
- Tunisian "snobbery" line is opinion, kept short.

- "Canada requires only the bottling country on the label" (Canada Food Insider) — false; CFIA says origin is not mandatory for oils.
- "Salov's lawyers admitted the oil isn't made in Italy" — a defence argument, not an admission.
- "Kirkland is certified by Bureau Veritas" — not on any Costco page; only tier-c sites. Script says "independent third party" and defers to filming the sticker.
