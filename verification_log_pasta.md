## 1. TITLE DECISION

The working title "$1 Pasta vs $5 Pasta in Canada — Can You Taste the Difference?" cannot be supported.
- No $1.00 regular-wheat pasta exists on any Canadian listing captured; the cheapest primary-source price is $1.25 (Dollarama, 450 g). The one sub-$1 index row (Fiorfiore Organic 500 g at $0.97, Walmart index) is anomalous and excluded.
- No test was performed by the channel; no blind, multi-brand Canadian test exists in the record. Foreign and laboratory sensory results cannot be transferred.
Delivered title: **$1.25 Pasta vs $5 Pasta in Canada — What Does the Extra $4 Actually Buy?** Every element is supported. The taste question is posed in the script and answered honestly as unproven in Canada.
