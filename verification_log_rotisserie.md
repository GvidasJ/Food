## MANDATORY PHRASINGS (binding on voiceover)

1. THE HONEST SPLIT at every full-price grocer: cooked usually cheaper PER BIRD, raw cheaper PER KILOGRAM, because spit birds are smaller. The script states it at Loblaws and Sobeys and generalizes it once — never claim "cooked is cheaper than raw everywhere." The documented unqualified case is Costco (CBC 2022 + current math).
2. NO taste commentary about any named store, positive or negative. Rankings and winners are price/weight/printed-label arithmetic only. The Narcity 10/10 is reported as their test, followed by the "we do math, not taste" line — that pairing is required.
3. LOSS-LEADER = LAWFUL, every time it's mentioned. The Competition Bureau framing stays; banned words for pricing: "scam," "trick," "rip-off," "caught." The Longo's delivery-app beat criticizes the *shopper's* math, not DoorDash's legality.
4. Every price airs with its date/source status: "at time of research," "on the flyer," "per reporting this year," "documented at" — and the shoot replaces or confirms each with filmed shelf tags and receipts. Any filmed price that contradicts the dossier OVERRIDES the dossier and the VO gets updated.
5. Sodium: printed values only, with serving size, no commentary. The Sobeys "lowest" claim keeps its "published figures we could find" qualifier; the Costco 350 mg keeps its "photographed label... we'll read the current bag" qualifier. US 460 mg never airs as Canadian.
6. Costco ingredients: ONLY via the company's attributed statement (carrageenan + sodium phosphate, "approved by food safety authorities") until the bag is filmed. The US preservative class action is EXCLUDED from the script entirely.
7. The tax twist must quote the mechanism precisely: heated cabinet = taxable; cold from refrigerated shelves to be reheated = zero-rated; raw always zero-rated. Provincial figures as scripted (ON/Atlantic/AB); NO Quebec number on air (QST bulletin unverified). Film hot + cold receipts as proof.
8. Sylvain Charlebois is NOT quoted in this episode (no on-record rotisserie quote exists). The on-record voices are: Galanti, Lyons, Munro, Rowe Farms (as reported), Longo (Rutgers), Boecker (Guelph), Owens (Arkansas). Names + affiliations stay attached.
9. No "shrinking chicken over time" claims — the only size fact is raw-vs-spit (1.6-1.8 vs ~1.2 kg, Rowe Farms) and uniformity (Owens/Lyons).
10. Supply management is mechanics, not editorial: pillars, 8-week meetings, farm-gate pricing, tariff percentages — attributed; the industry's "without government subsidies" line carries its attribution tag. No position taken for or against the system.
11. Company claims stay claims: RWA (Longo's/PC), "product of Quebec" (as observed in the dated test), St-Hubert's firsts, CFC's pitch.
12. Absence findings phrased exactly: Giant Tiger "we couldn't find a rotisserie chicken — their cooked chicken is packaged"; Whole Foods "no Canadian price published anywhere we could find."
13. Volume/lore attributed: 157.4M "by its own investor communications"; time-stamps/bell/weight-floor "American reporting says" until filmed in Canada.
14. Edit-week re-pulls: StatCan vectors (Aug 2026 CPI/prices mid-Sept), the RCI/Costco price range, and every retailer listing; recompute the $0.67 and +48% figures if inputs move.
