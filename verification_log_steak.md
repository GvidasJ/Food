## 1. TITLE DECISION

Working title kept: **Don't Buy Steak at These 5 Canadian Grocery Stores (Here's Why)**.
- Five banners are named (Costco, Metro, Walmart, Sobeys/Empire, Loblaw). Each carries at least one dated document giving a specific reason to check the label or weight before paying. The script states on air that the verdict is "check before you pay," not "never buy."
- No purchase, taste, or test is claimed. The only purchase implied is the viewer's own home-scale check, framed as the viewer's action.
- The order is by strength and date of documents (weakest to strongest), and the script says so, so Costco's thin record is disclosed rather than padded (the February video's Costco problem).
- Rejected framing: ranking by severity of story; "we tested"; any health, safety, pathogen or recall content; any statement that a blank flyer line is a rule breach (it is not: FDR B.14.018 applies only to carcass portions of 7 kg or more, verified live).

## 4. INTERPRETATIONS (compiler's readings, each flagged in the script by construction)

- "The front of that tray and the back of that tray can't both be right": the CFIA rule that imported beef carries the foreign grade and a "Product of" line means "Canada beef AAA" and "Product of USA / USDA Choice" are inconsistent on one package. This is a reading of the rule against the reporter's transcription of the label, not a finding against Costco; the script says "one package is not a pattern."
- "Only steak-specific enforcement in the file": the B.C. Superstore strip loin warning is the only regulatory action on a steak located across CFIA AMP pages, Measurement Canada (no per-retailer list) and the CBC record. If a new AMP is published before air, update.
- "Seven printed neither on a single one" / "Ten … on every steak": counts from the disclosure scorecard (Part 2 §4a) over the 23 flyer banners: both-on-all = Fortinos, Provigo, Sobeys, Safeway, IGA West, Farm Boy, Longo's, Co-op, Calgary Co-op, Nesters; neither-on-any = Maxi, Thrifty, IGA Quebec, Super C, Save-On, Urban Fare, T&T.
- "A printed triple A striploin cost between forty-two and fifty-five a kilo": Longo's 41.87 (rounded up to 42 in speech), Nesters 52.89, Sobeys 55.09. "An unprinted anything between twenty and fifty-seven": Super C 19.58 to Save-On 57.30 (per-pound lines only).
- "Five Empire banners in the weight record across two years": FreshCo (2024), Pete's Frootique (2025), Safeway, Thrifty Foods, Farm Boy (2026).
- "The line that says Canadian says double A, and the line that says triple A doesn't say Canadian": true of the online index copy only, and the script says so twice.
