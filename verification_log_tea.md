## VERDICT KEY
- **VERIFIED** — confirmed against a reliable secondary source (major outlet, Wikipedia with consistent corroboration, company's own materials for claims about itself)
- **VERIFIED-PRIMARY** — confirmed against a primary source (Justice Laws, Canada Gazette, CFIA/Health Canada/CRA pages, StatCan data, court/SEC-equivalent filings)
- **ATTRIBUTED** — a claim by a company/organization, presented in the script as their claim, not as fact
- **DERIVED** — arithmetic performed by us on verified inputs (inputs shown)
- **UNVERIFIED** — could not be confirmed; must NOT appear in the voiceover
- **EXCLUDED** — deliberately kept out of the script (reason given)
